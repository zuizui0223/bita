# BITA net-gain decomposition nonidentifiability v1

## Purpose

BITA distinguishes three quantities that are often conflated:

```text
R          recoverable compromise loss / benefit of dimensional access
K          added architecture or deployment cost
Delta_arch net architecture gain = R-K.
```

This note formalizes why a direct observed net worldline gap does not, by itself, identify `R` and `K` separately.

## Proposition 1 — additive decomposition nonidentifiability

Suppose a matched experiment identifies

```text
Delta = W_D* - W_S*.
```

and theory writes

```text
Delta = R-K
```

with `R>=0`, `K>=0`.

For any scalar `delta` such that

```text
R+delta >= 0
K+delta >= 0,
```

define

```text
R' = R+delta
K' = K+delta.
```

Then

```text
R'-K' = R-K = Delta.
```

Therefore a net architecture/worldline gap identifies only the **difference** unless an independent lane identifies either recoverable benefit or cost.

## Proposition 2 — release geometry does not automatically identify structural cost

An `x x y` surface can identify quantities such as

```text
x0*, x1*
R_state
preferential y loading
within-surface optimum fitness difference.
```

Those quantities can show contemporary functional release. But a structural maintenance/developmental cost that is common to all `y1` observations can act as an additive architecture offset.

Without an independent cost design or a comparator in which that cost is absent while the functional opportunity is otherwise commensurable, the decomposition into

```text
recoverable benefit R
vs
architecture cost K
```

is not unique.

## Proposition 3 — direct BALANCE/BITA ordering can be identified before the decomposition

The nonidentifiability above does **not** prevent direct worldline classification if total realized reproductive fitness is measured commensurably:

```text
Delta_W = W_D* - W_S*.
```

The sign of `Delta_W` can be directly identified even when `R` and `K` cannot be separated.

Thus:

```text
direct worldline order        may be identified
R/K decomposition             may remain unidentified
structural historical origin  remains a separate question.
```

This is why BALANCE can classify a direct middle world without first estimating `s,K`, while BITA requires additional evidence before promoting the result to an architecture-cost mechanism.

## What identifies the decomposition

Examples of valid additional information include:

1. an independent release benchmark that identifies `R` on the same fitness scale;
2. a direct deployment / maintenance cost manipulation or matched structural comparator identifying `K`;
3. a preregistered model in which one component is externally calibrated rather than inferred from the same net gap.

Local interaction curvature `kappa_delta`, a within-BITA optimum gain, or a nonsignificant cross-effect does not by itself identify `K`.

## Empirical consequence

A strong BITA result should report, separately:

```text
direct worldline gap
state-specific dimensional release
preferential loading
fitness consequence
cost semantics / provenance
whether R and K are separately identified.
```

## Claim ceiling

This is an identification statement about additive decomposition. It does not imply that all biological costs are constant offsets, nor that a direct worldline comparison is easy to construct. Non-additive, state-dependent, developmental and ecological costs can require richer models.
