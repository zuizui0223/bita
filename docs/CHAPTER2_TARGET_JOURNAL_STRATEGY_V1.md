# Chapter 2 target-journal strategy v1

## Decision

Primary target for the integrated SCH-sister BITA paper:

> **Ecology — Concepts & Synthesis**

This supersedes neither the old live submission package nor its portal files until the Chapter 2 candidate package is promoted. It records the journal fit of the new balance → differentiation → identification manuscript.

## Why Ecology / Concepts & Synthesis remains the best fit

The September 2026 Ecology guidance defines Concepts & Synthesis papers as articles that conceptually advance ecology, go well beyond the works reviewed, and provide new syntheses, directions or resolutions of old questions. The standard manuscript limit is 30 pages; submissions over 30 pages and up to 50 pages require a detailed cover-letter justification of both broad ecological contribution and the need for the extra length.

Current guidance:
- https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/19399170/author-guidelines
- https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/19399170/aims-and-scope/read-full-aims-and-scope

The Chapter 2 paper now has the correct C&S structure:

```text
long-standing ecological problem:
multifunctional traits face conflicting functional optima

new synthesis:
shared-axis balance
-> partial trait differentiation
-> causal mechanism identification

quantitative contribution:
Delta_arch = s L_S* - K
+ registered nonquadratic robustness

empirical synthesis:
partial differentiation / historical reorganization anchors
+ floral mechanism-identification worked case
+ 56/25 recurrence and 17-system fragmented frontier
```

The paper is therefore broader than a floral attraction/defence paper while retaining enough empirical biology to avoid becoming a detached mathematical note.

## Why The American Naturalist is the strongest alternative

The American Naturalist explicitly prioritizes broad conceptual unification across evolution, ecology and behavior and emphasizes innovative theoretical syntheses. It is therefore a serious alternative if the paper is revised toward a more evolution-first presentation.

Current guidance:
- https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/an/instruct
- https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/an/about

However, the present BITA manuscript compares optimized architecture states and deliberately does not model mutation, inheritance, branching, genetic covariance or transition dynamics. Its strongest distinctive second half is experimental mechanism identification rather than evolutionary dynamics. That currently makes Ecology C&S a cleaner fit.

A future American Naturalist version would be strengthened by at least one of:

```text
explicit evolutionary dynamics for architecture change
comparative transition-rate tests
experimental-evolution evidence
stronger genetic/developmental mapping of s or K
```

These are not required for the present Ecology C&S paper.

## Why Evolution is not the first target in the current state

Evolution welcomes theoretical investigations and syntheses that broaden understanding of evolutionary processes. Current guidance:
- https://academic.oup.com/evolut/pages/author-guidelines
- https://academic.oup.com/evolut/pages/why-publish

The topic is clearly evolutionary, but the current empirical ceiling is intentionally below a historical transition claim. Cichlids and *Dalechampia* establish relevant architecture states and reorganization, while the floral case identifies mechanisms after multiple axes exist. The paper does not yet show that a measured one-axis trade-off caused a particular differentiation event.

Thus Evolution becomes more competitive if the manuscript later adds direct comparative, phylogenetic or experimental-transition evidence. Without that addition, Ecology C&S better matches the current balance of ecological synthesis, theory and identification design.

## Submission order

```text
1. Ecology — Concepts & Synthesis
2. The American Naturalist — if the editor/readers prefer a more evolution-theory framing
3. Evolution — if direct evolutionary-transition evidence is added or requested
```

Ecological Monographs / Concepts & Synthesis is a fallback for a substantially longer synthesis, but the current plan should first try to keep the Ecology Main document at or below the 30-page standard target. The independent Chapter 2 candidate workflow records both the 30-page target state and the absolute 50-page Ecology C&S ceiling.

## Cover-letter hook for Ecology

The cover letter should not sell the paper as a new general theory of specialization. It should sell the bridge:

> Ecologists often measure compromises created by multifunctional traits, whereas specialization theory asks when functions can be partitioned across modules. We connect these levels by expressing the value of differentiation as the recoverable loss from a measured shared-axis compromise, discounting that value by incomplete decoupling, and then showing why the fitness interaction of the resulting traits still does not identify its ecological mechanism. The framework links balance, differentiation and causal identification in one empirical sequence.

If the candidate Main exceeds 30 pages, the required length justification should explicitly state:

1. why the balance → differentiation → identification bridge is a broad ecological contribution rather than a taxon-specific floral result;
2. why the two architecture figures, finite robustness result, empirical architecture-state anchors and floral mechanism-identification case are each required in Main to establish that bridge.
