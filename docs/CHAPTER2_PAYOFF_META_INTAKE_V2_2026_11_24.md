## Results
- Bernstein et al. 2012 (doi:10.1016/j.jbiotec.2011.10.001), section 3.2, reports 1.20±0.04 vs 1.04±0.02 g CDW/L, n=3 biological batch runs per arm. lnRR 0.143101; approximate delta variance 0.00049364; final biomass +15.38%. This is WT plus an engineered acetate specialist, not two strict specialists. It is a distinct experiment from Beck, but shares authors/lab and a related strain background. Not an additional eligible strict-split programme. Methods also inoculate per strain, so equal total starting density is not assured.
- Pande et al. 2014 (doi:10.1038/ismej.2013.211), Fig 2c: 43 of 54 tested cross-feeder genotype pairings significantly exceed WT 24h realized growth; 11 do not show this result, 9 of those involve the low-output ΔleuBΔnuoN genotype. Equal-ish initial densities, eight replicates per combination. In a separate selected direct competition experiment, 3/4 representative pairs exceed WT (n=10 each). These are design combinations with shared genotypes and controls, NOT independent programmes. Approximate source cost/benefit measurements: AA provision enables ~20% auxotroph competitive advantage; overproduction incurs ~5–8% cost. No pair-level numeric means or variance extracted; do not use a proportion meta-analysis, or interpret the 11 as negative effects.
- Dragoš et al. 2018 (doi:10.1016/j.cub.2018.04.046), Fig 4A: n=6 per arm, two-sided pooled-variance t-test p<0.04, positive root CFU/mm. Conditional on the reported test, t>2.3593, d>1.3622 and exact small-sample J-adjusted g>1.2569. This is a lower bound on the observed standardized estimate, not a confidence bound, point estimate or usable variance. WT is phenotypically heterogeneous; neither optimal-ratio pellicle comparisons nor other papers from the same research programme constitute independent new effects.
- Mridha and Kümmerli 2022 (doi:10.1111/jeb.14001): four iron contexts, direction +/unresolved/−/−; growth AUC, block-aware variance and WT shared covariance not recovered. Iron-replete benefit occurs when siderophores are not needed much; high limitation disadvantages specialists because the relative utility of tasks is asymmetric. Raw Dryad listing verified; no new raw data.
- Beck 2022 (doi:10.1128/msystems.00051-22): Fig 7–8 LAE estimates unchanged. Methods specify OD600 ~0.020 each producer and consumer versus ~0.020 WT, so initial total consortium OD is about twice WT. Source compares matched initial primary producer density, NOT matched total starting density. AAE had no superior performance metric; maximum AAE weak-buffer biomass over all four starting pH values was 0.12 (pH 7.5) versus WT 0.31 at pH 7.0. Thus AAE at pH 7.0 relative to WT at pH 7.0 is no more than ~0.39 from rounded reported means (conservative rounding upper bound 0.410), not an exact contrast or an effect with variance. Retain all initial pH and composition strata in next extraction rather than selecting only the favorable LAE pH 7 condition.

## Meta gate
Five candidate programmes considered. Two have endpoint biomass mean/SD/n, one is strict genetic specialization, zero have verified strict split AND equal-total-inoculum in this endpoint set. Two biomass summaries share a lab/authors; do not treat the pair as independent lab-level support. Do not pool growth, CFU, AUC or censored effects with endpoint biomass. No historical-origin contrast or cross-study pooled estimate.

## Prior art and actual novelty boundary
Harvey, Heys & Gedeon 2014, doi:10.1016/j.jtbi.2014.07.011 already prove a negative productivity result for unchanged pathway kinetics/yield in their model and explain experimentally observed benefits via improved efficiency. Beck and Dragoš already discuss environmental context and metabolic constraints. Chapter 2 cannot claim these in themselves as novel. Keep historical appearance of the architecture, realized benefit, accessibility, and maintenance separate; use their measured contrasts to test that theory's separate steps, not to relabel established payoff theory.
