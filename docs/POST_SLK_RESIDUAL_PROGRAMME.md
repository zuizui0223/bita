## Primary residual paper

Working thesis:

**Trait interaction is not ecological mechanism.**

Core claims to develop:

1. a positive multi-trait fitness interaction does not identify the ecological route generating it;
2. structural differentiation does not imply functional independence;
3. interaction detection should be followed by identified-set analysis, partial identification, selective intervention and separability diagnostics;
4. recurrent constituent biology can coexist with fragmented mechanism identification across the literature;
5. the empirical route-record synthesis should be used to map where identification is strong versus incomplete, not to claim prevalence of differentiation.
