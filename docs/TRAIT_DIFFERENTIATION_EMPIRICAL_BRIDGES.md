# Empirical bridges for the balance-to-differentiation programme

## Purpose

The Chapter 2 theory is intentionally more general than the current floral attraction/defence case. This file records external empirical systems that demonstrate the *states* represented by the architecture model without treating them as direct tests of the BITA threshold.

The evidential distinction is:

```text
architecture-state evidence
!=
causal test that one-axis compromise selected for the origin of the second axis
```

The current theory predicts when a differentiated architecture would outperform a shared compromise. Historical proof that this advantage caused a particular evolutionary transition requires additional evidence.

## Bridge A — cichlid oral and pharyngeal jaws: structural decoupling with residual integration

### Burress, Martinez & Wainwright 2020

**Decoupled Jaws Promote Trophic Diversity in Cichlid Fishes.** *Evolution*. DOI: 10.1111/evo.13971.

The system is unusually close to the general Chapter 2 architecture. A single feeding apparatus faces a mechanical force-motion trade-off. Ray-finned fishes possess structurally separate oral and pharyngeal jaw systems, partitioning prey capture and prey processing. In Neotropical cichlids, Burress et al. report reduced evolutionary integration between the two jaw systems relative to integration within a jaw apparatus and identify trait combinations that would be difficult under a single-jaw system.

Useful BITA mapping:

```text
shared architecture:
one jaw system must contribute to capture + processing

function-specific conflict:
force transmission versus motion / kinesis

differentiated architecture:
oral jaw -> mainly prey capture
pharyngeal jaw -> mainly prey processing

residual coupling lambda:
feeding ecology still produces correlated evolutionary responses
```

Admitted use:

> Structurally distinct trait modules can relax a multifunctional trade-off while remaining only partially evolutionarily independent.

Prohibited use:

> The study does not estimate BITA's `Delta_arch`, architecture cost `K`, or prove that the force-motion compromise caused the historical origin of pharyngeal jaws.

### Conith & Albertson 2021

**The cichlid oral and pharyngeal jaws are evolutionarily and genetically coupled.** *Nature Communications* 12:5477. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-021-25755-5.

This study is important because it prevents an over-simple modularity story. Across African cichlids, oral and pharyngeal jaw shapes remain evolutionarily correlated, with genetic evidence for shared control. Some specialized taxa deviate from the dominant integration pattern, but the broad system remains coupled.

Useful BITA mapping:

```text
presence of two structures
!=
complete functional independence

residual coupling / cross-talk can remain strong after structural differentiation
```

This is direct motivation for keeping `lambda` in the differentiated architecture rather than defining differentiation as perfect independence.

## Bridge B — Dalechampia: historical redeployment and addition of functional lines

### Armbruster, Lee & Baldwin 2009

**Macroevolutionary patterns of defense and pollination in Dalechampia vines: adaptation, exaptation, and evolutionary novelty.** *PNAS* 106. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.0907051106.

Phylogenetically informed comparative analyses of 81 taxa reconstruct repeated changes linking pollination rewards and defensive functions. The study reports 5–6 losses of resin reward followed by redeployment of resin for defense of male flowers in 3–4 lineages, correlated evolution among complementary defense lines, and repeated origins/additions of distinct defensive mechanisms. Five of seven inferred defense innovations were interpreted as exaptations.

Useful BITA mapping:

```text
function can move between pre-existing structures
new functional lines can be added rather than only optimizing one old line
historical architecture is contingent and partially reusable
```

Admitted use:

> Floral attraction and defense provide a historical example in which functional roles are repeatedly redeployed among structures and additional lines of defense accumulate.

Prohibited use:

> These transitions do not by themselves show that an optimized one-axis compromise was inferior to a two-axis alternative under the BITA fitness comparison.

## Bridge C — existing BITA floral identification corpus

The mature BITA floral work remains the within-project mechanistic bridge after multiple axes exist.

It contributes:

- a measurable two-trait interaction `Delta_AD W`;
- an identified set of compatible ecological channel allocations;
- partial-identification bounds;
- the crossed `A x D x antagonist x pollinator` intervention design;
- a four-way separability diagnostic;
- an independent remaining-channel assay;
- 56 source-adjudicated route records across 25 independent biological clusters;
- a 17-system fragmented identification frontier.

Its correct role under the new Chapter 2 is:

> once functions are distributed across more than one trait coordinate, how do we determine whether those coordinates actually relieve the original conflict and which ecological channels generate that relief?

It is not historical evidence for the origin of trait differentiation.

## Cross-system synthesis

The three evidence layers now form a coherent ladder:

```text
1. THEORY OF POSSIBILITY
   Rüffler et al. / Guillaume & Otto:
   specialization and reduced pleiotropy can evolve under functional trade-offs

2. ARCHITECTURE-STATE EVIDENCE
   cichlid jaws:
   structural decoupling exists but can retain residual integration

   Dalechampia:
   functions can be historically redeployed and new functional lines added

3. MECHANISM IDENTIFICATION
   BITA floral corpus:
   once two relevant trait axes exist, total interaction does not identify why
   the trade-off is relieved; crossed interventions are required
```

## Empirical ceiling for the current paper

A defensible current Chapter 2 can claim:

> The shared-versus-differentiated architecture comparison describes a real class of biological transitions: separate modules can expand otherwise constrained trait combinations, incomplete decoupling is common, and functional roles can be historically reassigned or multiplied. The current floral identification module then shows how to test the ecological mechanism of such multi-axis architectures.

It should **not** claim:

- a universal historical tendency from multifunctional traits toward modularity;
- that the new quadratic/power model is the first theory of specialization;
- that the floral `A x D` corpus reconstructs trait splitting;
- that structural separation guarantees evolutionary independence;
- that either cichlids or Dalechampia estimate the BITA differentiation threshold.

## Candidate manuscript use

Use cichlids and Dalechampia briefly in the Introduction/Discussion as empirical reality checks, not as a new meta-analysis. The paper's empirical quantitative core can remain the mature floral identification module, while these systems establish that the balance-to-partitioning problem is not flower-specific.
