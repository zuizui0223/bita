# Historical trait differentiation: source-grounded cross-programme synthesis (2026-11-24)

**Status:** completed first programme-level comparative evidence map, **11 distinct original research programmes and 13 source-frozen analytic receipts**. This is a *curated, exploratory evidence map* relevant to the historical SCH/BALANCE–SLK architecture-evolution Chapter 2. It is **not** a systematic literature sample, an effect-size meta-analysis, the active BITA mechanism-identification paper, or proof that a single universal trajectory caused each system.

## Central ecological result

**The origination of functional difference, its immediate collective value, ecological maintenance of complementary roles, and long-term genetic reversibility are empirically distinct stages.** The original-source programme receipts recover positive examples and counterexamples across those stages, but no evaluated study jointly identifies the entire `L → R → Phi → accessibility → invasion → fixation → occupancy` architecture trajectory on a comparable ancestral fitness scale.

This distinction is necessary even when the association between a specialist pair and a collective benefit is demonstrably positive: **short-run group performance need not imply evolutionary retention**, and **frequency-dependent coexistence need not imply group productivity exceeding an intact generalist**.

The evidence must be presented in the following four lanes, not one pooled "specialization advantage" figure.

### Pineau source-grade corrective analysis

The original 715-transfer evolution yields distinct source states in three of five independently started aerobic lines. Subsequent source measurements test **maintenance in an already-evolved PO-4 pair**: re-fitted original standard-oxygen Figure 2 competition frequencies give a zero-frequency-change point of **0.092551 Large** (eight source trajectories), whereas seven high-oxygen source competition series finish at mean **0.856454 Large**. Figure 2 caption high-oxygen *n*=6, Methods initial *n*=5, and the author's original sheet has seven complete labelled series; this discrepancy remains unresolved, without arbitrary exclusion.

Competitor-removal replay required stronger source QA. In original workbook `Fig5A`, Excel columns **6 (Large l1 Day40)** and **24 (Small s1 Day40)** duplicate **all 164 original (row, area value) measurements**. Both observations were **quarantined**. Among the resulting **five independent credible Day40 endpoints**, all three remaining Small replays increased biomass-weighted average radius (**+9.012 μm mean**), while both remaining Large replays decreased (**−5.976 μm mean**). The author workbook's raw isolated microscopy files and a separately authenticated publisher XLSX remain unavailable, so the two excluded columns are **not resolved**.

This source-corrected result supports context-dependent **phenotypic character displacement / maintenance**, not a new independent study, independent genetic origin count, or individual-cell functional module duplication.

## 2. Immediate group payoff versus maintenance and occupancy

Two independent microbial sources directly show why these outcomes cannot be collapsed.

**Bacillus EPS/TasA, Dragoš research programme (2018).** The genetically engineered EPS- and TasA-specialist mixture can yield increased short-term pellicle productivity at intermediate composition. However, the descendant `Δeps` specialist went extinct in **4 of 6 independently coevolved mixed cultures**. Independent evolved `tasA` variants also produced context-specific conditional roles and different dependence on BslA. This shows that beneficial engineered task separation does **not guarantee evolutionary maintenance**. The `Current Biology` source and subsequent `Nature Microbiology` source are **the same experimental programme** and must never be entered as two independent successful origin studies.

**Pseudomonas pyoverdine/pyochelin, Mridha et al. (2022)**, [DOI 10.1111/jeb.14001](https://doi.org/10.1111/jeb.14001). The engineered specialist mixture had a **higher WT-relative collective growth signal in iron-replete medium**, **unresolved difference in plain CAA**, and **lower WT-relative growth under two chelator regimes**; nevertheless the source supports negative-frequency dependence and fitted coexistence patterns. This establishes a *category distinction*: **frequency-dependent coexistence is not synonymous with a group-level productivity benefit**. Published source effects across four media are not four independent research programmes and not a calibrated cross-paper architecture payoff.

Kim's M+D role-reconstitution additionally supports short-run complementarity and ~10% M mixture equilibrium, but not long-term heritable module retention.

## 3. Genetic exit and reversal: the state after division is not permanent

**Chen et al. (2024),** [DOI 10.1073/pnas.2317182121](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2317182121), is a directly evolved **reverse** architecture example. Engineered *Pseudomonas stutzeri* Degrader and Detoxifier specialists regained an autonomous whole-pathway genotype in **5 of 12 alternating carbon-regime evolution lines**, versus **0 of 12** in constant mutualism and **0 of 12** in constant competition. Sequencing gives five *independent successful lineages*, not 20 origins from four sequenced isolates per lineage; genetic reacquisition is consistent with homologous recombination / horizontal transfer. This is not another case of forward specialization.

Bacillus co-evolution supplies a second *distinct* demonstration of partner-loss / dependency escape, albeit with engineered rather than historically spontaneous specialist genotypes. The pertinent biological contrast is between **current functional association** and **genetic evolutionary persistence**, not a universal pooled "exit rate" across Chen and Bacillus.

The total number of programmes in the matrix is **11**: three emergence, Bacillus one engineered-partner evolution, Chen one reverse-autonomy, and five boundary/context programmes. The categories are **for organizing source evidence**, not a predeclared representative sample. Furthermore, the three emergent systems do not share a common phenotype, selection protocol, or reproductive mode.

**Do not divide any of these counts by 11 as if the 11 programmes were a random sample of all evolution research**. Absence of a claim in a source grade is not proof the biological process never occurs; in some cases the required endpoint was not measured. There is no defensible single pooled effect size because “successful divergence”, “colony inhibition”, “pellicle CFU”, “growth AUC versus WT”, “autonomist reassembly”, “functional loss detectability” and “clone relative respiration rate” are different causal estimands and nonexchangeable experimental units.

## Manuscript-ready Results passage (for historical Chapter 2 / SLK provenance, not active BITA)

> Across eleven intentionally selected, original-source-adjudicated experimental evolution and functional reconstruction programmes, we separated evidence for the emergence of differentiated phenotypes, immediate collective payoff, ecological maintenance, and evolutionary escape. Three programmes documented the appearance of new phenotypic or functional specialist roles, but only one provided independent starting-population denominators for both emergence and failure: in a long-term aerobic snowflake yeast experiment, three of five original populations evolved divergent Small and Large lineages (Pineau et al., 2024). These lineages showed oxygen-sensitive within-study competition, and competitor-removal replays moved in the opposite direction for the two phenotypes: source-valid Small populations increased in size (three populations; mean +9.01 μm), whereas Large populations decreased (two populations; mean −5.98 μm). The remaining Day40 pair was excluded because the deposited workbook contained 164 identical original measurements under two distinct population labels. In separate programmes, engineered Bacillus specialist communities exhibited short-term group benefits yet lost one partner in four of six coevolved cultures, while Pseudomonas specialist lineages regained autonomy under alternating ecological conditions (five of twelve lines versus none in either constant treatment). These findings demonstrate why functional differentiation, current ecological benefit, maintenance, and evolutionary reversal are distinct empirical questions. They do not estimate a common probability of specialization or identify the historical cost of an optimized shared functional architecture.

Use "distinct empirical questions", not "universally causal independent stages". No empirical grand mean is calculated from incompatible effects. A reader should be able to trace every admitted numerator and denominator to the single corresponding original research programme.

## Remaining mechanism-identification boundary and the right next question

The historical architecture theory seeks the *shared-coordinate loss* `L`, the recoverable differentiated-state benefit `R`, and the full entry/maintenance barrier including `K(u)`. **These quantities are not measured on a matched common ancestral fitness scale** in these selected programmes. The remaining direct empirical test must independently quantify the two task-specific performance surfaces of the same ancestor, an accessible evolved or engineered differentiated alternative, startup costs, genotype-specific fitness, and long-run lineage state transitions. The proof/test distinction remains: an observed specialization is compatible with the theory but is not a retrospective causal test of the precise theoretical origin mechanism.

**Publication ownership:** SCH identifies functional conflict; SLK owns architectural advantage and evolutionary transitions; BITA's **active** manuscript owns why a trait interaction does not identify its ecological mechanism. This evidence map is retained as a candidate cross-programme Results/Discussion provenance attachment to historical Chapter 2/SLK, without changing any active canonical paper or proceeding to journal submission.
