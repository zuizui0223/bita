# BITA Pedicularis structural-y promotion contract v1

## Why this extra gate is needed

The current Pedicularis Chapter-2 experiment uses:

```text
y0 = water drained
y1 = water retained.
```

That is a strong **causal functional-state intervention**. It can establish that the water defence releases the exsertion conflict.

It is not yet sufficient to claim that evolution has produced a second **trait coordinate**. Water presence is a state of the cupulate-bract system, not itself a heritable structural phenotype.

Therefore Pedicularis has two distinct Chapter-2 lanes:

```text
Lane A — functional-state release
water OFF/ON
-> causal antagonist protection
-> x-optimum release

Lane B — structural trait differentiation
continuous water-holding / retention phenotype y
-> preferential functional loading
-> x-y dimensionality / partial modularity.
```

Lane A can reach outcome-level dimensional release. Lane B is required before using Pedicularis as a strong trait-differentiation example.

## Existing structural measurements

The published geographic study measured the cupulate-bract architecture directly, including:

```text
width of top of cupulate bract
width of bottom of cupulate bract
cupulate-bract height.
```

It also defined exsertion as:

```text
x = (flower length - bract height) / flower length.
```

This creates an important coupling problem: bract height contributes mathematically to x. Therefore bract height alone must not be declared an independent y coordinate.

## Preferred structural y

The first-choice candidate is a functional phenotype measured independently of x:

```text
y = standardized water-holding capacity / retention performance.
```

Operational measurements may include:

```text
maximum retained water volume (mL)
water depth at a standardized fill volume
retention duration / half-life after standardized filling or rainfall
leakage / evaporation rate
fraction of the ovary/corolla base submerged through the oviposition window.
```

Cup dimensions may be used as mechanistic predictors of y, but y should be measured directly whenever feasible.

## Stage Y0 — establish repeatable y variation

Across plants / genotypes / populations, measure x and y repeatedly.

A structural-y candidate must show:

```text
repeatable among-plant variation
within-plant measurement reliability
variation not created solely by transient rainfall amount
sufficient range to test function.
```

Do not promote y from a one-time water-level snapshot.

## Stage Y1 — distinguish y from x

Because x and cup morphology share structure, report their coupling explicitly.

Required analyses:

```text
x-y correlation / covariance
within-population and among-population components
partial association of y with function 2 after conditioning on x
partial association of x with function 1 after conditioning on y.
```

Strong correlation does not automatically invalidate differentiation, but it lowers the claim from near-independent modules to **partial functional modularity**.

## Stage Y2 — preferential functional loading

A structural y must predict antagonist protection after controlling x:

```text
higher y
-> lower early attack / oviposition
-> lower seed predation
-> higher viable seed survival.
```

At the same time, its pollination-facing cross-effect must remain inside a prospectively declared tolerance:

```text
y -> pollen receipt / legitimate pollinator handling
small enough to preserve preferential loading.
```

Non-significance alone is not sufficient. Use equivalence / bounded cross-effect criteria.

## Stage Y3 — intervention or natural experiment

The strongest contemporary trait-coordinate result manipulates or otherwise exogenously changes y while preserving x.

Possible future approaches include:

```text
standardized water-volume / retention manipulation that does not alter x
reversible sleeve / reservoir manipulation around the cupulate bract
genotype / population contrasts with matched x but divergent retention capacity.
```

Any intervention that changes bract height and therefore changes x at the same time cannot point-identify y as a second coordinate.

## Relationship to the current water OFF/ON experiment

The current acute drainage experiment remains valuable and should run first.

```text
water OFF/ON positive
-> defence function is causally real
-> D2 functional-state release can be tested.
```

Then structural-y promotion asks whether natural or experimentally manipulable variation in water-retention phenotype behaves as a distinct axis.

Thus:

```text
FUNCTIONAL_Y_STATE
!=
STRUCTURAL_Y_TRAIT
!=
HISTORICAL_MODULE.
```

## Bottom line

Pedicularis can already test whether a second **functional state** releases Chapter-1 compromise. To make Chapter 2 a strong trait-differentiation paper rather than only a defence-state experiment, add a structural-y lane centred on standardized water-holding/retention performance and quantify its residual coupling to exsertion.
