# BITA quantitative Results/Discussion audit V1

## Purpose

This audit translates the frozen quantitative claim ledger into Results/Discussion language. It does not upgrade total interaction to mechanism identification and does not reinterpret literature-audit counts as natural prevalence.

| Qualitative claim | Quantitative claim licensed | Ceiling / not licensed |
|---|---|---|
| The constituent ecological routes recur across the screened literature. | `56 directional route records` occur across `25 independent biological clusters`; the high-information frontier contains `17 systems`. | These are literature-structure counts, not an estimate of natural route prevalence or effect magnitude. |
| Full mechanism allocation remains unclosed in the screened frontier. | `0 systems` complete the full allocation design plus an independent remaining-channel assay. | This licenses a fragmentation claim for the screened evidence set, not a claim that point identification is impossible in principle. |
| The Kessler anchor supports a positive total interaction at Level-1. | `A1 approximately +0.200 to +0.240`, while `A0 approximately -0.030 to +0.030`; therefore `Delta_AD W=A1-A0` remains positive under the registered aggregate constraints. | This supports **Level-1** positive interaction. Because `A0` remains zero-compatible, strict **Level-2/3** release is not identified. |
| A total interaction does not uniquely identify ecological channel allocation. | `I(delta)={(rho,iota,kappa): rho-iota-kappa=delta}` is the compatible allocation set. | `CHANNEL_ALLOCATION = NOT_POINT_IDENTIFIED_FROM_TOTAL_INTERACTION`; a precise total interaction is still not a measured mechanism decomposition. |
| Biological restrictions can sharpen identification. | If independently justified `kappa_delta >= 0`, then `rho_delta-iota_delta = Delta_AD W + kappa_delta >= Delta_AD W`. | This is a conditional partial-identification bound. It is not an empirical estimate of `rho_delta`, `iota_delta`, or `kappa_delta` without the restriction and additional data. |
| Crossed consumer interventions can add identifying information. | Selective `A x D x antagonist x pollinator` contrasts can shrink the compatible set when baseline and separability conditions are satisfied. | Point identification still requires the declared assumptions plus an independent remaining-channel assay; subtraction alone does not measure `kappa_delta`. |

## Discussion ceiling

```text
NATURAL_PREVALENCE = NOT_ESTIMATED
CHANNEL_ALLOCATION = NOT_POINT_IDENTIFIED_FROM_TOTAL_INTERACTION
RHO_DELTA_POPULATION_DISTRIBUTION = NOT_ESTIMATED
IOTA_DELTA_POPULATION_DISTRIBUTION = NOT_ESTIMATED
KAPPA_DELTA_POPULATION_DISTRIBUTION = NOT_ESTIMATED
STRICT_LEVEL2_PREVALENCE = NOT_ESTIMATED
STRICT_LEVEL3_PREVALENCE = NOT_ESTIMATED
```

Results may therefore quantify the interaction anchor and the structure of the evidence base. Discussion may argue for partial identification and targeted follow-up experiments, but must not turn `56/25/17`, the Kessler aggregate ranges, or `Delta_AD W` into a natural prevalence estimate or a unique ecological mechanism.
