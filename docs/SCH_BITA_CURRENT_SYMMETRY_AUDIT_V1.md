```text

```

```text
z_P* = argmax W10(z)
z_G* = argmax W01(z)

It requires convergent support from distinct state optima, an interior combined optimum, opposite optimum shifts when each functional demand is removed, and opposing functional-component gradients near the combined optimum.

## Main evidence asymmetry

The chapter pair should not be forced into artificial empirical equivalence.

**SCH currently has stronger direct evidence for heterogeneous one-axis evolutionary responses**: stabilizing compromise cases, context-dependent maintenance, experimental/population redirection, and shared-cue mechanisms.

**BITA currently has stronger formal differentiation machinery and a positive real-world architecture anchor**: Peucedanum shows partial functional differentiation and a selection mosaic, while cichlids and Dalechampia provide broader architecture-state precedents. But canonical causal `R_state` remains unmeasured.

This asymmetry is legitimate. The decisive dissertation-level result is to close both in one biological system.
