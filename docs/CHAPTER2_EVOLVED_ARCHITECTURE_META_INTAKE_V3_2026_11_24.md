# Chapter 2 v3 — realized differentiation, performance, retention (2026-11-24)

## New origin contrast
Poltak & Cooper (2011), doi:10.1038/ismej.2010.136: six structured-biofilm and six planktonic founding populations, all from a common clonal ancestor. All 6 structured populations evolved three derived morphs; 0 of 6 planktonic populations did so. One planktonic population did evolve a single novel S-like morph. Strict three-morph contrast: RD +1.0, Wilson–Newcombe 95% CI [0.448,1.000]; two-sided unpaired Fisher p=0.0021645. The broad 'any new morph' sensitivity is 6/6 vs 1/6: RD +0.833, 95% CI [0.277,0.970], p=0.01515. These are distinct outcomes, not a correction of one into the other. Regimes differed in selection, transfers and effective generation totals; this does not identify a pure causal coefficient of physical structure or ancestral functional conflict. Two focal evolved populations had greater mixed-cell yield than any constituent, but mixed biomass was lower than that of the high-biofilm specialists. Group size, biomass, and fitness are distinct. Do not count B1/B2 or their constituent morphs as independent publications.

## Evolved payoff control
Yang et al. (2020), doi:10.1128/AEM.00051-20, revisited the Helling 1987 / Rosenzweig experimental population. Three evolved ecotypes from the same founder history have complementary resource use. Evolved monocultures do not show significantly higher biomass than ancestor; two-member mixes do, and the three-member mixture has an author-described almost twofold median biomass vs ancestor. Fig 4 reports medians/quantiles and 4–6 biological replicates, not exact means/SD/n by arm. No fabricated lnRR or variance. Pair and triple mixtures share ancestry and controls; Helling and later analyses count as one historical programme. Table 2 residual metabolites supply a separate mechanism observation: E3 alone has acetate 36.85±4.30 μM (mean±SEM), mixes E3+E1 and E3+E1+E6 have no detected acetate. Do not interpret nondetection as exact zero or compute log response ratios. Steady-state chemostat biomass not silently combined with batch endpoint biomass.

## Opposite sign / retention
Saxer et al. (2009), doi:10.1098/rspb.2008.1827: 12/12 originally well-mixed populations evolved multiple colony morphotypes by generation 350. Nine original founder histories were assayed in paired ecological disruption (3 excluded because morphotypes could not be reliably distinguished). Imposed spatial structure reduced colony-size diversity by about half and changed the frequency-dependence slope (paired t=-10.21, df=8; descriptive d_z=-3.4033). This is a maintenance contrast, not another 9 founding-origin experiments, and the 50% is a continuous diversity metric, not 50% extinction. Neither nine paired assays nor substrate treatments are independent studies. This supplies a counter-direction to a universal claim that structure promotes differentiation: structure can support spatial niches, yet disrupt metabolite-mediated dependencies. The conceptual mechanism-dependent reversal is explicitly prior art in Saxer; the research's novelty must be tested against it.
