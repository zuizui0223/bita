# Mechanism → Pattern → Identification bridge

## Purpose

The canonical paper preserves the original Mechanism → Pattern logic while distinguishing **recurrence**, **partial identification**, and **point identification**. At the coarsest empirical level, the original bridge remains:

```text
Mechanism
→ constituent ecological recurrence
→ identification coverage
→ mechanism-allocation experiment
```

The refined inference sequence is:

```text
Mechanism
→ constituent ecological recurrence
→ total-interaction identified set
→ fragmented identification frontier
→ assumption-indexed partial identification
→ selective mechanism-allocation experiment
```

## Layer 1 — constituent ecological recurrence

Retained source-adjudicated synthesis:

```text
56 directional route records
25 independent biological clusters
A → pollination:  5
A → antagonism:   8
D → antagonism:  18
D → pollination: 10
same-system multi-route: 14
context/sign-switch:     17
```

These overlapping counts establish recurrence capacity of the biological ingredients. They are not natural-prevalence estimates and do not estimate `Delta_AD W`, `rho_delta`, `iota_delta`, `m0_delta`, or `kappa_delta`.

## Layer 2 — total interaction and partial identification

For a measured total interaction

```text
Delta_AD W = delta
```

the compatible channel allocations satisfy

```text
I(delta) = {(rho, iota, kappa): rho - iota - kappa = delta}.
```

The total interaction therefore identifies a set rather than a unique mechanism. Explicit restrictions or channel-specific observations can shrink that set. The principal recovered bound is

```text
kappa_delta >= 0
=> rho_delta - iota_delta >= Delta_AD W.
```

This is an assumption-indexed bound on the biotic balance, not an empirical claim that kappa is always non-negative and not a universal attraction–defence theorem.

## Layer 3 — fragmented identification frontier

The 16-system high-information audit asks which dimensions of the allocation problem existing studies already constrain.

Current anchors occupy complementary faces:

- Kessler et al. 2008 — closest trait-factorial side;
- Egan et al. 2021 — complementary consumer-factorial side;
- Soper Gorden & Adler 2018 — observational A×D plus randomized context modification;
- Sun & Huang 2015 — selective-access defence-system anchor.

Screened-set facts:

```text
independent joint-cost assay:        0
full rho/iota/kappa point ID:        0
```

The stronger pattern is **design fragmentation**: relevant biological and experimental information exists, but it is distributed across different studies. The next-study question is therefore which additional intervention or measurement most shrinks the remaining identified set.

## Layer 4 — point identification

Point identification requires the selective

```text
A × D × antagonist × pollinator
```

design, explicit `m0_delta` handling, and the `A×D×G×P` four-way separability diagnostic. A non-zero four-way value rejects the simple separable-channel representation. Any residual joint channel remains `U_delta` until independent A×D cost/allocation evidence validates its interpretation.

## Main inference

```text
constituent channels recur
AND
existing studies occupy complementary identification faces
AND
a total interaction can support explicit partial bounds
BUT
full channel allocation remains not point-identified
THEREFORE
use the smallest informative augmentation where possible,
and the fully crossed selective design when point identification is required
```

## Non-negotiable boundary

The recurrence layer may support the statement that the framework's biological ingredients are not peculiar to one system. It may not be used to validate the algebra, estimate channel interactions, imply natural prevalence, or establish a universal sign. Partial-identification statements are valid only under their declared restrictions. Residuals are not mechanisms by definition.
