# Mechanism → Pattern → Identification bridge

## Purpose

The canonical identification-design manuscript keeps the original Mechanism → Pattern research logic, but separates two empirical questions that must not be collapsed.

```text
Mechanism
→ constituent ecological recurrence
→ identification coverage
→ mechanism-allocation experiment
```

## Layer 1 — constituent ecological recurrence

The retained source-adjudicated mechanism-route synthesis contains:

```text
56 directional route records
25 independent biological study clusters
A → pollination:  5 clusters
A → antagonism:   8
D → antagonism:  18
D → pollination: 10
same-system multi-route: 14
context/sign-switch:     17
```

These overlapping counts show that the biological ingredients represented by attraction, pollination, antagonism and defence recur across independent systems. They are evidence-capacity/recurrence statements within the screened architecture, not natural-prevalence estimates.

This layer does **not** estimate:

- `Delta_AD W`;
- `rho_delta`;
- `iota_delta`;
- `m0_delta`;
- `kappa_delta`.

Marginal `A→P`, `A→G`, `D→G`, and `D→P` evidence therefore cannot be promoted to cross-trait channel interactions.

## Layer 2 — identification coverage

The stricter 16-system high-information audit asks whether the recurrent biological ingredients have been combined in designs that can allocate a total attraction-by-defence interaction.

Current anchors:

- Kessler et al. 2008: closest trait-factorial side;
- Egan et al. 2021: complementary consumer-factorial side;
- Soper Gorden & Adler 2018: observational `A×D` plus randomized interaction-intensity modification;
- Sun & Huang 2015: selective-access defence-system anchor.

Current screened-set endpoint:

```text
independent joint-cost assay:      0
full rho/iota/kappa identification: 0
```

These are screened-set coverage results, not estimates of literature prevalence.

## Main inference

The cross-system result is therefore not simply “nobody has studied the mechanism.” The constituent mechanisms recur, and sophisticated trait or consumer experiments exist. What is missing is their intersection on the same `A`, `D`, consumer-intervention and outcome coordinates.

The intended inference is:

```text
constituent channels recur
AND
trait-factorial / consumer-factorial information exists
BUT
joint channel allocation remains unidentified
THEREFORE
next experiment = selective A × D × antagonist × pollinator design
                  + m0_delta characterization
                  + separability diagnostic
                  + independent joint-cost assay
```

## Non-negotiable interpretation boundary

The recurrence layer may support the statement that the framework's biological ingredients are not peculiar to one system. It may not be used to validate the algebra, infer a universal interaction sign, estimate channel interaction magnitudes, or claim that 56/25 is a prevalence sample.
