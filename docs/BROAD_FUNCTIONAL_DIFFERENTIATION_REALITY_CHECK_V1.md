# Broad functional-differentiation reality check v1

## Purpose

BITA is now framed as a general Chapter-2 problem: when does a phenotype move from one shared multifunctional coordinate toward more functionally differentiated coordinates?

This audit asks whether the lower-level ingredients of that architecture occur in real plants outside the narrow attraction-defence factorial. It is a reality check, not a claim of historical modularization.

## 1. Floral scent component partitioning

Kessler et al. (2013; DOI `10.1111/ele.12038`) used transgenic *Petunia x hybrida* lines in which specific components of the floral volatile bouquet were silenced. The experiments showed that some floral scent compounds contribute to host location by florivores while other compounds reduce florivore damage.

The key architecture is:

```text
one complex bouquet
-> component-specific ecological functions.
```

Admitted role:

```text
DIRECT_COMPONENT_FUNCTION_DIFFERENTIATION
WITHIN_DISPLAY_PARTITIONING
```

Claim ceiling: the study does not reconstruct an ancestral single multifunctional compound that split into descendant modules, and the same experiment does not estimate a complete pollinator-versus-florivore common-fitness surface for every component.

## 2. Floral versus extrafloral nectar as spatial functional separation

Floral nectaries primarily reward pollinators, whereas extrafloral nectaries (EFNs) attract ants and other arthropods that can defend plants against herbivores. The EFN literature documents repeated origins, large structural diversity, and strong evidence for ant-mediated protective function. Reviews also emphasize that floral and extrafloral nectaries can share nectar-secretory machinery while differing in location, developmental context, chemistry, regulation, and ecological role.

This provides a useful architecture-level analogy:

```text
shared resource class = nectar
spatially differentiated structures:
  floral nectary      -> pollination function
  extrafloral nectary -> defence mutualism.
```

Admitted role:

```text
REAL_WORLD_SPATIAL_FUNCTIONAL_DIFFERENTIATION
REPEATED_EVOLUTION_OF_A_SECOND_FUNCTIONAL_LOCATION
```

Claim ceiling: EFNs have evolved repeatedly and are not generally homologous to floral nectaries. Therefore this is **not** evidence that one ancestral shared nectary split into floral and extrafloral modules. It supports the plausibility and repeated utility of spatially separated trait architecture, not D5 historical modularization.

## 3. Conditional gating of shared signals

The existing SCH/BITA evidence spine contains *Biscutella* systems in which a shared volatile cue is induced after florivore attack and differs among populations in inducibility. This is not full trait duplication. It is a regulatory route to reduce simultaneous multifunctional conflict by changing when the cue is expressed.

Admitted role:

```text
TEMPORAL_STATE_DEPENDENT_FUNCTIONAL_SEPARATION
PARTIAL_MODULARITY_WITHOUT_DUPLICATION
```

## 4. Route separation in floral defence

The BITA mechanism synthesis recovers a recurrent qualitative rule:

```text
separated effective domains
-> antagonist filtering can preserve pollination

overlapped domains
-> pollinator interference is more likely

bypassable domains
-> defence fails to carry the intended antagonist-reduction function.
```

This is real-world evidence that preferential functional loading can be produced by spatial, temporal, chemical, or geometric architecture even when the two traits are not developmentally independent modules.

Admitted role:

```text
FUNCTIONAL_LOADING_DEPENDS_ON_EFFECTIVE_DOMAIN_SEPARATION
```

## 5. Attraction x defence reproductive surface

Kessler et al. (2008) remains the strongest existing local two-trait outcome anchor. Benzylacetone and nicotine were experimentally crossed in all four combinations and reproductive outcomes were measured. Registered aggregate-compatible reconstructions preserve a positive `A x D` interaction sign, with the attraction effect under the defended state positive while the low-defence attraction effect remains close to and spanning zero.

Admitted role:

```text
LOCAL_TWO_TRAIT_FUNCTIONAL_RELEASE_ANCHOR
```

Claim ceiling: source/design-based uncertainty for the interaction and a clean flower-restricted defence scope remain unresolved; the study does not start from a directly identified Chapter-1 shared-trait optimum.

## Generalization result

Real plants repeatedly implement lower-level forms of functional differentiation through:

- component partitioning;
- spatial separation of functionally similar secretory structures;
- conditional / temporal gating;
- geometric or access-domain separation;
- two-trait reproductive complementarity.

These observations support the biological plausibility of the BITA architecture. They do not yet establish the complete cross-chapter chain:

```text
ancestral or experimentally identified shared compromise
-> preferentially loaded x and y
-> dimensional release
-> mechanism-resolved gain after all added costs
-> historical modularization.
```

The immediate BITA target remains contemporary D0-D3 functional differentiation. Historical D5 modularization must remain a separate claim.
