# Final submission audit — saturated Mechanism → Pattern candidate

## Audit purpose

This audit checks whether the active PR #129 candidate tells one coherent **fixed theory + saturated mechanism-Pattern synthesis** story, whether the registered scientific and expansion gates have closed, and whether empirical evidence is promoted beyond what its designs identify.

## Integrated submission spine

The manuscript-facing repository retains one fixed theoretical target:

```text
For one declared floral attraction trait A,
one declared flower-specific antagonist-reducing trait D,
and one declared outcome scale W,
the local A x D mixed partial can be interpreted mechanistically only after
channel definitions and orientation conditions have been established.
```

After the orientation gate:

```text
W_AD = rho - iota - kappa
```

with antagonist relief (`rho`), mutualist/pollinator interference (`iota`), and direct joint-cost curvature (`kappa`) kept distinct.

The empirical synthesis does not estimate this mixed partial by pooling unrelated marginal studies. It asks which component mechanisms recur, which change state across ecological contexts, which co-occur in the same systems, and which quantities remain empirically unidentified.

### Gate A — direct interaction

The registered direct `A x D` search reached its stopping rule. One strict direct cluster is retained, Soper Gorden & Adler (2018), *Impatiens capensis*. Its two reproductive-component interaction estimates have confidence intervals overlapping zero and opposite point directions. The direct sign is unresolved rather than positive or negative.

### Gate B — four marginal mechanism families

All four theory-facing route families have explicit source-adjudicated states. The expansion broadens their independent biological coverage while retaining the same focal-route and flower-specific-D definitions.

### Gate C — quantitative and contextual syntheses

Two modules are locally reproduced quantitative syntheses:

- **Leal et al. 2025:** female reproductive success LRR `-0.210` (48 independent clusters), nectar standing crop `-0.483` (28), legitimate visitation `-0.291` (22), with extreme heterogeneity and declared sensitivity diagnostics retained.
- **Sasidharan et al. 2023:** 32 conservative study components; physiological florivore 84/103 versus pollinator 151/220; assembled RD `+0.129`; LOCO positive 32/32; only three paired both-role components and all paired differences zero.

Three additional syntheses are retained with explicitly different status:

- **Haas-Desmarais et al. 2026:** 171 studies / 1,348 cases; publisher supplement package independently retrieved and hashed; no local raw-effect reanalysis claim and herbivory is not focal D.
- **Caruso et al. 2019:** published main analysis 755 gradients with SE from 36 articles; Dryad metadata and workbook identities verified; file-byte access currently blocked; selection gradients are not W_AD.
- **Junker & Blüthgen 2010:** 18 publications / 425 observations; visitor dependence supports consumer filtering but is not identical to pollinator-versus-antagonist roles.

Their study/observation counts are not added to route-ledger N and their scales are not pooled with the two reproduced modules.

### Gate D — conditionality

Seventeen independent sign/state-switch clusters plus seven context-only programs now document structured conditionality. Beyond the original trait intensity, resource/exposure, consumer identity, response stage, and compound/mechanism axes, the saturated Pattern includes:

- guarded defence;
- spatial / temporal / attack-mode filtering;
- visitor functional-mode routing;
- lifecycle-stage role reversal;
- population/site and trait-class dependence.

Incompatible outcomes are not forced into a cross-outcome meta-regression.

### Gate E — same-system architecture

Fourteen source-adjudicated systems contain at least two theory-relevant marginal routes. Same-system recurrence is not relabelled as direct `A x D` evidence. Representative states include shared signal tracking, guarded defence, attack-mode filtering, visitor routing, and response-stage dependence.

### Gate F — direct joint cost

The registered joint-cost search remains saturated at zero strict eligible estimates. Therefore `kappa` is **unidentified**, not estimated as zero. Separate attraction costs, defence costs, trait covariance, and ecological pollinator interference are not combined to manufacture `kappa`.

### Gates G-H — robustness and inference boundary

Module-specific dependence, influence, source-version/access, sensitivity, and bias diagnostics are retained at the level appropriate to each data structure. The theory–empiricism boundary forbids treating marginal routes as `W_AD`, study counts as parameters, context-program counts as extra route N, secondary-synthesis counts as route N, deposit proportions as prevalence, or the single direct interaction as a general sign result.

## Decision on reproductive assurance R

`R` remains only an auxiliary background moderator of the pollination-mediated channel. A paired audit showed that changing `R` from `0.0` to `0.5` changes the local sign in **16 of 1,296** otherwise matched scenario × response-shape evaluations. Retaining it transparently preserves the canonical sensitivity results.

`R` is not a third focal trait, an omnibus reproductive-strategy axis, or a separate empirical target. The manuscript must not claim a **three-trait theory**.
