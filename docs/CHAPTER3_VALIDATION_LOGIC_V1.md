# Chapter 3 / BITA validation logic v1

## Core claim
BITA asks when additional trait dimensionality actually releases a system from the shared-coordinate compromise and which ecological channels make that differentiated state work.

## Theory layer
The architecture boundary is

```text
Delta_arch = R - K
```

and in the quadratic bridge

```text
R = s L_S*
Phi = sL - K.
```

The theory therefore predicts when differentiation should be favoured as a function of conflict load, recoverability/decoupling and added architecture cost.

## Causal validation layer
The decisive empirical test differs from SCH and BALANCE. BITA manipulates / measures two trait coordinates and asks whether functions load preferentially onto them and whether the old compromise is released:

```text
x x y
```

with function-specific consumer/state manipulations where needed.

Key estimands include:

- state-specific dimensional release `R_state` toward a frozen SCH reference;
- preferential functional loading of `x` versus `y`;
- optimized total-fitness gain from access to the second axis;
- decoupling `s` and an independently justified cost `K` when available;
- crossed mechanism decomposition for antagonist relief, pollinator interference and joint channels in the floral worked case.

## Mechanism-identification layer
The presence of two traits does not itself identify differentiation. BITA requires evidence that the second coordinate actually carries a function and that release/fitness gain is produced through identified ecological channels.

This is why local `A x D`, `Delta_AD W`, loading tests and crossed interventions are used after the architecture-level question.

## Generality layer
Universality is sought as recurrence of the **differentiation architecture** across different biological systems, not recurrence of one floral mechanism.

Comparative anchors may include jaw systems, floral modules, sex-specific flower classes or other traits, provided they show partial/complete functional separation under residual coupling and cost.

## What BITA does not test
BITA does not by itself prove the historical origin of modularity. A contemporary differentiated state, even causally identified, is not evidence for an ancestral shared-to-differentiated transition without independent historical/developmental data.

## One-line validation question
**Does adding or exploiting a second trait dimension move the original trait out of its SCH compromise, improve fitness, and assign the competing functions to different coordinates through identifiable ecological mechanisms?**
