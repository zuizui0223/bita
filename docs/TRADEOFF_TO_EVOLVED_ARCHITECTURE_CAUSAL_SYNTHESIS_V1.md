# From functional trade-off to evolved trait architecture: a cross-repository causal synthesis (2026-10-19)

**Status: source-grounded integration and prospective falsifiable predictions; NOT a claim of experimentally identified structural origin.**

## One focal question, not four interchangeable hypotheses

> Under what ecological, structural and evolutionary conditions does a verified conflict among functions using the same trait coordinate cause a transition toward differentiated trait architecture?

The word **cause** requires three logically independent links:

1. **Ecological conflict:** selective demands pull on the same phenotypic coordinate in opposing directions under identified conditions.
2. **Architecture advantage and realizability:** an alternative with extra functional dimensions yields a *net* benefit on the same fitness scale, AND a heritable path can invade or cross the relevant barrier.
3. **Evolutionary origin:** the differentiated structure arose after, and in response to, the relevant conflict rather than by prior exaptation, correlated selection, ecological sorting or drift.

Contemporary conflict + differentiated morphology, even observed in one species, does not supply link 3. A positive trait interaction does not supply link 2.

### Species are not single conflict/architecture states

**Polemonium viscosum:** original studies identify self-versus-outcross pollen interference on a reproductive timing/spacing coordinate AND, separately, bee-attraction-versus-ant-defense pressure on an integrated corolla-shape coordinate. These are distinct function pairs, tissues, experimental contexts and sources. The co-occurrence does not prove that one conflict produced the architecture observed in the other.

**Narcissus assoanus:** forced self/cross interference affected greenhouse seed set, but its tested interference did not yield a detected male/female fitness cost in the wild. Potential conflict under forced overlap must not be treated as naturally realized conflict.

These make a nontrivial comparative prediction: architecture must be modeled at **species (or lineage) x function-pair x tested trait coordinate x exposure regime**, never by a species-wide binary 'trade-off present'. Source: [BALANCE functional-pair multiplex](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/main/docs/BALANCE_PLANT_CONFLICT_MULTIPLEX_ECOLOGY_V1.md).

## First new independent species-first morphology/opportunity baseline: AusTraits v7

The complementary [BALANCE public-trait data PR #249](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/pull/249) has now completed an **actual source acquisition**, not merely a plan. Its checksum-verified AusTraits **v7.0.0** archive (DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.15718081](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15718081)) contains **1,798,215** long-format records, of which **46,299** records for six predeclared structural-sex, perianth, and pollination traits represent **24,492 source taxon names**. The original archive digest and non-redistributed aggregate receipt are frozen in [the BALANCE live readout](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/data/austraits-independent-architecture-20261019/docs/BALANCE_AUSTRAITS_V7_LIVE_READOUT_20261019.md).

A strict no-fuzzy-join comparison retained **2,841 binomial species** with exactly resolved source labels for species sex system (`hermaphrodite`, `monoecious`, `dioecious`) and pollination syndrome (`insect` or `wind`). Of 2,495 insect-labelled species, **183/2,495 = 7.33%** had separated-sex flowers/individuals; of 346 wind-labelled species, **60/346 = 17.34%** did. The raw wind-minus-insect difference is **+10.01 percentage points**.

This is **independent morphology/opportunity ecology**, NOT independent SCH-confirmed opposing functional optima or evidence of new module origin. Critically, only **seven genera** are represented under both pollen-mode categories (among 603 genera), so the within-genus / repeated-transition evidence needed to distinguish deep phylogenetic sorting from ecological selection is nearly absent. The dataset is also unevenly contributed: `Sjostrom_2006` supplies 20,832 of 27,014 source sex-type records, while `White_2020` and `Draper_2023` dominate pollination-syndrome records. A post-open source-stratified sensitivity has now been completed and separately labelled: among **1,720** exact species with one contributing source-dataset key per trait, the `Draper_2023` pollen-source cohort retains **667** insect and **94** wind species with separated-sex organizations at **2.70% vs 14.89%** (wind minus insect **+12.19 percentage points**); the `White_2020` cohort has **821** qualifying insect and **zero** strict wind species and cannot provide a mode contrast. The post-open seven overlapping genera all have a **zero recorded genus-level separation-rate contrast**, reinforcing the weak phylogenetic common-support ceiling. These post-open diagnostics do not turn the raw association into historical selection evidence. The [BALANCE source-sensitive report](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/data/austraits-independent-architecture-20261019/docs/BALANCE_AUSTRAITS_V7_LIVE_READOUT_20261019.md) preserves original-vs-postopen analysis status and archived execution provenance.

**Further actual botanical hierarchy and congeneric-discovery result (separate post-open exploration):** [BALANCE's verified hierarchy and lineage report](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/data/austraits-independent-architecture-20261019/docs/BALANCE_AUSTRAITS_V7_STRUCTURE_LEVEL_AND_LINEAGE_V1.md) distinguished whole-plant sex-system architecture from flower-level bisexuality/unisexuality. In **1,080** species with both source labels, 797 had a shared bisexual flower, 98 had sex functions separated among flowers, 112 among individuals, and 73 had partial or discordant labels. Among **425** species with unambiguous direct flower structural sex and insect/wind annotation, unisexual flowers occurred in **37/383 insect (9.66%)** versus **10/42 wind (23.81%)**, an unadjusted **+14.15 percentage-point** association. However, **zero genera** are shared between these two pollination-mode groups in the flower-level sample, and **no source-provider pairing** clears the prespecified two-arm sample/genera support gate. There is no licensed within-genus environmental or fitness inference.

A full-name-consistency congeneric screen of the same v7 source data then recovered **11 genera** containing at least two species with shared reproductive sex systems and at least two species with flower/individual sex separation, from **15,936 qualified species across 1,720 genera**. Examples include *Lobelia*, *Wurmbea*, *Acacia*, *Melicope*, and *Denhamia*. By contrast, the directly flower-level sex-state source has **1,499 qualified species from 1,386 genera** and **zero** genera with the required two species on both the bisexual/unisexual sides. **None of the 11 organism-level candidate genera includes both strict wind and insect pollination states** in the qualified records; *Lobelia*, *Wurmbea*, and *Denhamia* do contain different recorded sex systems among insect-labelled species. These are useful **comparative candidate taxa**, NOT 11 inferred transitions, 11 phylogenetically independent contrasts, or evidence that a historical ecological trade-off selected new architecture.

The ecological synthesis should therefore ask first **which function pair and ecological setting differentially favours alternate reproductive organizations inside one documented lineage**, not treat a broad pollination-mode correlation as the antecedent conflict causing origin. That more exact causal question still awaits functional fitness estimates, accepted-name phylogenies and developmental/heritable architecture measurements.

**Updated claim ceiling:** the four-repository programme has (a) conflict-source patterns, (b) existing structural architecture observations, and now (c) an independently acquired multi-species **opportunity/morphology denominator**. It still lacks a dataset linking **measured within-axis conflict strength -> costed differentiating architecture -> historical lineage transition**. Do not turn this new raw cross-species association into the missing evolutionary causal proof.

## Priority final causal-identification system from the independent genus frontier: Wurmbea

The independent AusTraits congeneric screen identified *Wurmbea* as a candidate (14 strict hermaphroditic vs 3 sex-separated species, with insect pollination annotated for both organizational groups). A targeted original-source check, independently written to [BALANCE's Wurmbea causal handoff](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/data/austraits-independent-architecture-20261019/docs/BALANCE_WURMBEA_FOCAL_CAUSAL_BRIDGE_20261019.md), finds **actual ecological intervention evidence**, not merely the coexistence of trait states:

- **Ramsey et al. 2006, *W. dioica***: experimentally increased outcrossing of fruiting males reduced females' reported relative **seed-fitness advantage from 3.7 to 1.4**, suggesting mating context can alter the relative benefit of maintained sex morphs (DOI 10.1111/j.1420-9101.2006.01129.x). This is a sex-specific *component payoff*, not a costed net architecture margin or observed new architecture arising.
- **Ramsey et al. 2006, *W. biglandulosa***: self/cross and morphological manipulation studies observed very high inbreeding depression, providing the candidate ecological route by which separating male/female functions can differ in reproductive return (DOI 10.1111/j.0014-3820.2006.tb01134.x, also legacy identifier 10.1554/04-433.1 for the **same** paper).
- **Vaughton & Ramsey 2011, *Annals of Botany***: high/low resource manipulation altered gender plasticity of subdioecious males (66% versus 42%) and located plasticity costs in only particular sex-system contexts (DOI 10.1093/aob/mcr163). This is a plausible real-world mechanism for persistence of plastic shared functions rather than irreversible specialization.
- **Case et al. 2008**: published plastid phylogeny explicitly leaves ancestral sexual-system nodes and historical transition directions uncertain, and documents the failure of simple species-monophyly assumptions for the two focal species (DOI 10.1086/523368).

The resulting **nontrivial but bounded** biological inference is that even within a predominantly insect-pollinated reproductive system, the relative seed-fitness payoff of segregated sex function can depend strongly on mating and selfing context, while the genetic/developmental architecture may remain plastic. The full SCH `L`, structural SLK `Phi=R-K`, frequency-dependent PAYOFF invasion, and historical causal origin all remain **unidentified**. This is the strongest *focal validation candidate*, not an independently derived macroevolutionary proof or substitute for the independently sampled floristic background.

## New actual population-genetic source test in Wurmbea: close plastid proximity, not causal origin

An independently executed [BALANCE Wurmbea GenBank PR #250](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/pull/250) restored **all 22 ndhF accessions** for 22 original Australasian voucher/sexual-state records from Case et al. (2008, DOI [10.1086/523368](https://doi.org/10.1086/523368)) and **all 23 original deposited focal locus accessions** for six `MR1–MR4/AC53/AC38` samples. The resulting source-validated [primary QC report](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/analysis/wurmbea-genbank-history-20261019/docs/BALANCE_WURMBEA_CASE2008_REAL_GENBANK_VOUCHER_QC_RESULT_20261019.md) documents a **critical original source metadata conflict**, not only genetic distances.

- `W. biglandulosa` MR2 (**M**) and MR4 (**D**) match at **0 differences /1,628 aligned comparable A/C/G/T sites** across `ndhF` and `atpB-rbcL`, the **only two source-voucher unambiguously aligned loci** for this pair. Table A1 lacks MR4 `trnLF`. An originally calculated 3-locus 0/3,709 result was **retracted from primary interpretation** after checking the ENA voucher metadata.
- `psbBTNH` paper Table A1 assigns `EU044681` to **MR2** and `EU044680` to **MR3**, while ENA headers say exactly the **opposite**. **Both are HOLD**, not silently corrected. A separately labeled **post-open ENA-voucher assignment sensitivity** yields MR2–MR4 **1 mismatch /3,709 aligned sites over three loci**, not complete cpDNA identity.
- The source-voucher-concordant `W. dioica` AC53 (**M**) versus AC38 (**D**) comparison has **16 differences /5,004 aligned sites** across four plastid regions. The audit reports **21/23** focal deposited sequences with matching paper and ENA source-voucher identities; all 23 sequences were downloaded.
- The original publication's **minimum five sexual-system changes** is a *source-published phylogenetic inference*, with uncertain direction/rooted identity, NOT our new estimate. Our new data are **voucher-level alignments**, not a nuclear species phylogeny, evolutionary transition model, nor independent confirmation of causal origins.

A **newly cross-audited physiological-versus-evolutionary alternative** is particularly important: Barrett, Case & Peters (1999), DOI [10.1046/j.1365-2745.1999.00336.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-2745.1999.00336.x), reported that **about half of marked W. dioica plants that expressed as males in 1981 but flowered again in 1983 produced hermaphroditic flowers**; females retained their phenotype in the monitored returners. The wider population's female frequency remained ~49%, while male and hermaphroditic categories shifted. Vaughton & Ramsey's controlled high/low resource study, DOI [10.1093/aob/mcr163](https://doi.org/10.1093/aob/mcr163), independently demonstrated high/low resource sensitivity of sexual expression in subdioecious males (66% vs 42%). Therefore **sexual expression can shift within a living plant**, and a phenotypic M/D label need **not** identify a new inherited architecture or an independent phylogenetic transition. The controlled experiments and historical phylogenetic comparison remain different estimands with different source biological units.

Thus actual alternative sex architectures can be found in populations with close plastid genealogical backgrounds; trait structure and neutral maternal genetic genealogy need not be separated identically. **That still does not establish that measured intraorganism sexual-function conflict induced the origins.** The functional payoff-side source remains the conditional female seed-fitness advantage **3.7 versus 1.4** under manipulated outcrossing. It crosses the *illustrative* nuclear `T=2` but not maternally inherited `T=1` fitness threshold. Neither inheritance law is estimated for Wurmbea, and there is no source-proven `K` or `Phi`.

The newly narrowed identification target is explicit: compare **genetically supported, viable shared and sex-separated architectures** with male and female contributions on **one fitness scale**, estimate seed/pollen-mediated differences under the same ecological intervention, and infer historical transitions with uncertainty-aware population nuclear markers rather than equating plastid similarity with a new origin.

## New residual PAYOFF result: permanent differentiation versus reversible plasticity

The separate [PAYOFF plasticity/modularity PR #338](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/pull/338) adds a **third comparator** missing from a simple shared-versus-differentiated value test: a reversible, cue-dependent shared phenotype. It does **not** relabel dichogamy, herkogamy, gender plasticity or a plant-level sexual system as the same biological construction without independent evidence.

The exact synthetic two-function model decomposes the potential cost of one shared coordinate into

```text
C = ab/(a+b)*Delta²                      unavoidable simultaneous functional conflict
T = (a-b)²*Delta²/[4(a+b)]              extra cross-environment static mismatch
LOSS_SHARED = C+T
LOSS_PLASTIC* = minimized cue-adjusted C+T+B+per-change switching debit
LOSS_FIXED_MODULES = K                   idealized fully decoupled two-coordinate cost
```

Its **two different falsifiable phase slices** are:

1. **Perfect cue still cannot remove concurrent conflict:** with `q=1`, no switching or plastic machinery cost, `LOSS_PLASTIC*=C`. Increasing the weaker *simultaneously active* functional demand (`b`) at constant stronger demand (`a`), trait-optimum gap, and module cost `K` can flip the predicted optimum from plastic to fixed modules. A declared synthetic witness has exact `b*=3/11`. This connects directly to the ecological question of **overlap in time / anatomical access of the function pair**, not mere opposite signs in observed selection gradients.
2. **Fast switching can defeat plasticity:** in a *separately declared* noisy-cue two-state Markov model, cue accuracy `q`, true environment switch probability `rho`, and phenotype-reconfiguration cost are distinct. One fully specified exact witness has `rho*=0.65625`, with PLASTIC best at `rho=.1` and FIXED best at `rho=.9`. The analytic result is exact **only within a memoryless symmetric cue-response policy class**; a better memory-based controller might change the boundary.

Both boundaries were verified with separate unit tests (including exhaustive 16-case environment/cue enumeration). These are **model-only theoretical conditional statements**, not evidence of Wurmbea genetic transitions, and not a claim of mathematical novelty against all previous plasticity theory. Importantly, the model's FIXED architecture coexpresses two independent functional coordinates; it is **not automatically a correct model of separate male versus female individuals**.

**Observed comparator direction is context-dependent even within the same Wurmbea study.** A single original high/low-resource and pollen-allocation study (Vaughton & Ramsey, DOI 10.1093/aob/mcr163) reports pollen **per flower** in subdioecious *W. dioica* phenotypic males at ~**2× hermaphrodites**, but phenotypic males in cosexual and gynodioecious *W. biglandulosa* at about **0.6× hermaphrodites**. Increasing female-function expression correlates *negatively* with pollen/flower size only in the subdioecious population, versus nonnegative or positive measures in the other two source populations. This biological variation supplies a useful **context-specific functional-conflict candidate**, not a causal `C`, `K`, a direct same-coordinate pure-function optimum or total reproductive fitness. Plant-size differences and one-population-per-sexual-system dependence remain. [Source-specific BALANCE receipt](https://github.com/zuizui0223/balance/blob/analysis/wurmbea-genbank-history-20261019/docs/BALANCE_WURMBEA_FUNCTIONAL_COST_HETEROGENEITY_V1.md).

**Revised cross-repository causal discriminator:** before studying historical structure origins, independently measure (i) how much of the observed conflict is **simultaneous** rather than sequential across environments, (ii) whether the actual shared phenotype shows reversible cue-dependent response and how costly that response is, (iii) whether a separately realizable fixed structure changes *total* rather than one-sex fitness, and (iv) whether the candidate is heritable/invasible and historically postdates its attributed selective conflict.

**Existing real source limitations remain explicit:** the 1981/1983 marked-plant Wurmbea gender changes and 66%/42% manipulated-resource plasticity observations support reversible phenotypic response; they do **not** identify `q`, `rho`, `C`, `T`, switching cost or `K`. The mating manipulation's 3.7 vs 1.4 female seed advantage does not identify `Phi` or genetic invasion. BALANCE U2 timing/space and U3 sex/pollen-routing architecture codes provide candidate strategy types, but no response is relabeled PLASTIC without a repeated reversible phenotype assay. The first practical empirical comparison still needs source-level data on all three candidate architectures, measured conflict, shared timescale and realistic genetic inheritance. [Theory, proof, model assumptions and counterexamples](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/blob/theory/plastic-compromise-to-modules-20261020/theory/PLASTIC_COMPROMISE_VERSUS_FIXED_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Theoretical counterexamples already implied by the registered models

These numbers are **synthetic mathematical examples**, not measured fitness.

**A. Conflict without positive differentiation value (shared compromise persists).** For the registered quadratic model with `a=b=1`, pure-component optimum gap `Delta=2`, and residual coupling `c=1`: `L=2`, `s=1/3`, `R=sL=2/3`. If a new differentiated architecture costs `K=0.8`, then `Phi=-0.133333... <0`. Conflict exists, but the shared architecture has higher intrinsic optimized fitness.

**B. Higher intrinsic value without invasion.** Keep `L=2`, `s=1/3`, but set `K=0.5`: `Phi=+1/6`. If the population game has positive architecture coordination feedback `eta=0.3`, rare differentiated invasion has `I_D=Phi-eta=-2/15`. The new architecture is globally advantageous on the frequency-independent comparison but cannot invade at rarity under this declared game.

**C. Positive full module-release gain despite selection against infinitesimal release.** This is a **different baseline**: start from a pre-existing partially coupled *two-axis* architecture (`a=b=1`, `Delta=2`, reference coupling `c0=1`) and consider weakening the coupling edge by `d in [0,1]` at linear cost `0.9d`. PAYOFF's exact thresholds give `k_local=4/9`, `k_global=4/3`. Since `4/9 < 0.9 < 4/3`, full edge release gains `13/30` units, while the infinitesimal marginal net gain is `4/9-0.9 < 0`. This is a **finite-jump barrier**, not `R=sL` computed from the original one-axis architecture.

The executable synthetic guards are in the [PAYOFF integration branch tests](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/blob/integration/tradeoff-architecture-causality-20261019/tests/test_tradeoff_architecture_causal_counterexamples.py) and existing [modularization barrier model](https://github.com/zuizui0223/payoff/blob/main/src/modularization_barrier.py). These test model implications, not a biological causal transition.

Mechanism-specific effective-domain differentiation, not dimensionality alone, predicts functional relief** | BITA selective consumer factorial / real route-resolved fitness outcomes; actual joint cost assay | Added axes remain seen by the same agents and no channel-specific benefit is recovered |
| **P5. Historical origin follows and tracks conflict** rather than only current co-occurrence | Reconstructed ancestral architecture and conflict/environment sequence, phylogenetic independent transitions, genetic/developmental feasibility, contemporaneous net benefit | Module predates conflict; same transitions occur in matched conflict-free clades; alternative selection explains transitions |

For P1, define a **same-scale architecture x conflict interaction** (both architecture options viable and evaluated under both conditions):

```text
Gamma = [W_D*(conflict-on)-W_S*(conflict-on)]
      - [W_D*(conflict-off)-W_S*(conflict-off)]
```

`Gamma>0` under randomized conditions would support conflict increasing the *relative fitness payoff* of the differentiated alternative. This still does not establish historical origin. `W_D*` and `W_S*` must be optimized or appropriately matched under a predeclared feasible-domain definition; simply comparing extant species with different structures invites ancestry and ecological-confounding bias.

1. **Freeze a species/lineage-first sampling frame before reading the focal architecture result.** Stratify by phylogeny and habitat, record study effort and missingness, and include conflict-null/unknown cases.
