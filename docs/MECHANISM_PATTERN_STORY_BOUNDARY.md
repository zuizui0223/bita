# Mechanism → Pattern story boundary

This note fixes the integrated scientific story now carried by PR #129. It adds no estimate and changes no evidence count.

1. `W_AD = rho - iota - kappa` is a bookkeeping decomposition, not the novelty.
2. Under non-negative joint-cost curvature, `W_AD > 0` implies antagonist relief exceeds pollinator interference: complementarity is confined to a one-sided selectivity window. Across the declared 2,592 evaluations there are zero counterexamples, while about 23% of in-window evaluations remain substitutable; the window is necessary, not sufficient.
3. The floral-larceny synthesis shows that the antagonist-pressure gate can be open on average but is not universal: female fitness LRR is -0.210 across 48 independent clusters, 35/48 are negative, the 95% prediction interval is -1.13 to +0.71, and declared moderators explain only 0-8% of heterogeneity.
4. The reward → visitation → female-fitness sequence is constituent-path evidence, not a demonstrated within-study mechanism: only five clusters measure all three outcomes, two show all three negative, and the shared reward-visitation subset has `r = -0.17`.
5. Direct joint-cost curvature `c_AD` remains unmeasured. A sufficiently negative value is the only failure route for the one-sided bound in the declared family, so its sign is the minimal empirical applicability/falsification gate. A 2 × 2 allocation experiment can test that sign; a full attraction × defence factorial remains necessary to estimate total `W_AD` and decompose its mechanism.

The paper therefore supports a one-sided mechanistic theorem plus a recurrent but context-dependent empirical Pattern. It does not claim a universal positive or negative `W_AD`, a two-sided universal selectivity criterion, a demonstrated reward-mediated larceny chain, or an empirical estimate of `c_AD`.
