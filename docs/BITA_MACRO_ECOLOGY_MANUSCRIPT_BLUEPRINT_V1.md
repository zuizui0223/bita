# BITA macro-ecology manuscript refocus blueprint v1

## Paper identity

BITA is refocused as a **macro-ecological synthesis of floral defence selectivity and interaction routing**.

Primary question:

> When can a flower reduce antagonistic use without paying the same cost in legitimate mutualistic function, and what ecological axes cause that selective window to open, close, or be bypassed?

Primary candidate rule:

> **effective access/exposure domains**, rather than a fixed chemical-versus-physical defence class, organize the realised interaction state.

## Main Results structure

### Result 1 — matched same-D systems recover a selectivity pattern

Use the 17-system matched-D registry as the causal/comparative core.

Current direct Stage-2 pattern:

```text
SEPARATED:  2 preserved/improved, 0 impaired
OVERLAPPED: 0 preserved/improved, 1 impaired

+ 4 separated systems with null-compatible pollinator contrasts
+ 4 transition systems
+ 1 explicit bypass/null-D boundary
```

Do not oversell n=3 as a final meta-regression. Present it as the strict evidence subset embedded in the larger state-structured corpus. The frozen exact gate gives Fisher p = 0.333; the null-compatible sensitivity gives p = 0.143. The three strict systems also perfectly confound domain relation with broad modality (two separated physical systems versus one overlapped chemical system), so `domain > modality` is explicitly not a current result.

### Result 2 — selectivity is dynamic within systems

Promote the inherited 8-cluster defence-side conditionality result to the main paper.

Main ecological message:

```text
dose / expression
exposure duration / reward context
consumer identity
response stage
temporal expression
        ↓
same D moves between guarded and interfering states
```

The ordered exposure subset supports a selective-window interpretation: antagonist response can occur at lower exposure than strong pollinator interference, and increasing exposure closes the window.

### Result 3 — floral geometry reroutes cheating across a community

Use the Sakhalkar 2023 public network as the independent macro-scale test.

BITA reanalysis:

```text
18,440 source rows
14,383 after source-script visiting exclusion
183 visited plant species
57 cheating-exposed species with tube length

rho(tube length, robbing-vs-thieving balance) = 0.346786
permutation p = 0.0086

median tube length:
robber-only = 2.0893
thief-only  = 0.6766
```

Interpretation:

> longer / less directly accessible flowers do not simply remove cheating; they shift exploitation toward bypass by robbing rather than thieving through the legitimate opening.

This gives the paper a true community-scale ecological result rather than a warning about inference.

## Introduction logic

1. Flowers must remain accessible to mutualists while limiting exploiters.
2. Defences and access barriers are often described by trait class, but the same class can be selective, costly, or ineffective.
3. The missing general question is **what determines selectivity**, not merely whether defence has a cost.
4. BITA predicts that realised outcomes depend on whether mutualists and antagonists occupy separated, overlapping, transitional, or bypassable effective domains.
5. Test this prediction at three scales: matched same-D systems, within-system state transitions, and an independent multispecies interaction network.

## Discussion logic

Lead with the ecological rule:

> floral defence is not intrinsically safe or costly to pollination; its outcome depends on who encounters the trait, how strongly, through which route, and at what stage.

Then connect the three scales:

```text
same-D comparisons
    show selective vs costly states

within-system transitions
    show the selective window opening/closing

community network
    shows geometry rerouting exploitation mode
```

Only after these results discuss identification limits.

## Claims to avoid

- “all separated defences preserve pollination”;
- “chemical versus physical defence is irrelevant everywhere”;
- “the Sakhalkar association is a causal defence experiment”;
- “n=3 strict Stage-2 systems establish a universal meta-regression”;
- “14,383 exactly reproduces the paper's stated 14,391 visits”;
- “Kessler nectar absence is unambiguously a conventional defence trait”;
- prevalence claims from the literature corpus.

## Current paper-level conclusion

> **Across matched floral systems, within-system exposure transitions, and a multispecies visitor network, access and exposure structure repeatedly predicts whether antagonistic use is suppressed, mutualistic function is retained or impaired, and exploiters switch to bypass routes. Floral defence selectivity is therefore an ecological state of trait × consumer × context rather than a fixed property of a defence class.**
