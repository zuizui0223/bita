# BITA figure rebuild plan — mechanism-identification Main

Status: `RENDERED_FIGURES_STALE / CAPTIONS_REFROZEN / REBUILD_REQUIRED`

The previous five Main figures were designed for the integrated architecture-plus-mechanism paper. They must not be reused unchanged after the canonical manuscript refocus.

## Figure 1 — outcome hierarchy

Purpose: make the first inferential split visually unavoidable.

Required panels:

1. four-cell `A x D` outcome table;
2. `A0`, `A1`, and `Delta_AD W = A1-A0`;
3. three nested regions: Level 1 interaction relief, Level 2 constraint release, Level 3 strict reversal;
4. one counterexample showing `Delta_AD W > 0` while both `A0` and `A1` remain negative.

No architecture-value quantities should appear.

## Figure 2 — identified-set geometry

Purpose: show why interaction precision is not mechanism identification.

Required panels:

1. plane `rho-iota-kappa=delta`;
2. effect of `kappa>=0` restriction;
3. effect of a bounded independent `kappa` assay;
4. effect of measuring one consumer channel.

This should be the conceptual centerpiece of the paper.
