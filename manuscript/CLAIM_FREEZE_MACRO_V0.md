## Active thesis

> **Floral defence selectivity is an ecological state generated by unequal access and exposure of antagonists and mutualists, and floral access architecture can reroute exploitation rather than simply eliminate it.**

The paper is not a warning paper whose headline is trait interaction != mechanism.

The previous identification result remains an inferential boundary and secondary contribution.

## Active mechanistic spine

Use the effective-exposure model:

\[
z_H=q_Hx,\qquad z_P=q_Px
\]

with thresholds

[
x_H^*=	au_H/q_H,qquad x_P^*=	au_P/q_P.
]

Selective window:

\[
x_H^*<x<x_P^*.
\]

Permitted qualitative predictions:

- separated effective domains can widen the selective window;
- overlap can narrow or close the selective window;
- bypass/tolerance can eliminate focal antagonist-reduction efficacy;
- increasing dose/exposure can close a selective window;
- access restriction can reroute cheating toward bypass/robbing.

Do not claim universal numerical q or tau values.
