## 6. Reference-use rules for the refocused paper

Use the framing reviews/meta-syntheses to establish what is already known: herbivore and pollinator effects can interact; floral scent can filter visitor classes; herbivory can alter pollination; several ecological agents impose selection on floral traits.

Do not use those syntheses as matched-D replications.

Use primary matched systems only for claims supported by the focal source-adjudicated trait and outcome.

Use Sakhalkar 2023 for the independent community-scale access-routing result. Do not count its individual visits or plant species as matched-D study clusters.

Use Kessler 2015 as a consumer-context / legacy A×D bridge. Do not double-count it as a new primary matched-D system.
