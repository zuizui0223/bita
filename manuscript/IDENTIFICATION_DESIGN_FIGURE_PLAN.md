# Identification-design figure hierarchy

This plan replaces the theorem-led result hierarchy for the candidate identification-design manuscript. Existing frozen submission figures remain untouched until the candidate manuscript is accepted as the new canonical text.

## Main Figure 1 — What a total A×D interaction does and does not identify

Purpose: establish the inference problem before any algebra.

Panels:

A. Four-cell two-level trait factorial defining `Delta_AD W = W11 - W10 - W01 + W00`.

B. Three distinct latent allocations that can generate the same total `Delta_AD W`: antagonist relief, pollinator interference, and an unallocated joint channel.

C. Observational boundary: `Delta_AD W` alone identifies the total interaction, not its channel allocation.

Main message: **interaction detection is not mechanism identification**.

## Main Figure 2 — Crossed intervention design and internal falsification

Purpose: make the new contribution operational.

Panels:

A. The 16-cell `A × D × antagonist access × pollinator access` design.

B. Antagonist-exclusion contrast yielding `rho_delta` at each pollinator state.

C. Pollinator-presence increment yielding `iota_increment_delta` at each antagonist state, with separate `m0_delta` baseline correction.

D. The single `A×D×G×P` four-way coupling: rho cross-P invariance and iota cross-G invariance are the same contrast up to sign. Non-zero coupling rejects the simple separable channel representation.

Main message: **the design estimates channels and tests the assumption that makes those channel labels meaningful**.

## Main Figure 3 — The joint-cost assay and sign diagnostic

Purpose: prevent kappa from being defined as a residual.

Panels:

A. Crossed-design outputs produce an unallocated residual `U_delta`, explicitly not labelled kappa.

B. Separate `A×D` allocation/construction-cost assay under standardized biotic conditions estimates `kappa_assay_delta`.

C. Residual-versus-assay outcomes: agreement, disagreement/missing channel, and scale-incommensurate sign-only comparison.

D. Diagnostic case: if `Delta_AD W > 0` while `rho_delta <= iota_delta`, the remaining joint channel must be negative; interpreting it as kappa requires the independent assay.

Main message: **simple algebra becomes useful only after independent measurements constrain the hidden channel**.

## Main Figure 4 — Existing studies occupy complementary halves of the design

Purpose: connect the framework to real ecology without pretending that marginal routes estimate the channels.

Core comparison:

- Kessler et al. 2008: strong experimentally crossed A×D-like trait factorial, positive discrete interaction sign under published aggregate constraints, but no crossed selective consumer toggles and systemic-D caveat.
- Egan et al. 2021: crossed herbivory × pollination consumer factorial, but no independently manipulated A×D floral trait factorial.

Secondary anchors:

- Soper Gorden & Adler 2018: observational A×D + randomized agent-intensity modification; all retrofit target intervals unresolved.
- Kessler et al. 2015: 2×2 floral phenotype, but second axis is reward rather than D; current publisher supplement has no obvious machine-readable source data.
- Sun & Huang 2015: selective flower-associated D mechanism, but no A manipulation.

Visual form: design-coverage matrix rather than a study-count bar chart.

Main message: **the missing empirical object is the intersection of trait factorial and selective consumer factorial, not the absence of either literature by itself**.

## Main Figure 5 — Experimental roadmap

Purpose: end with an executable biological design rather than a conceptual summary.

Flow:

1. choose independently manipulable A and flower-specific D;
2. choose a system permitting selective antagonist and pollinator interventions (body size, route, diel period, phenology, or other access asymmetry);
3. cross A×D×G×P;
4. measure pollinator-independent baseline `m0_delta`;
5. test the four-way separability contrast;
6. run an independent A×D cost assay;
7. classify outcome as channel-resolved, non-separable, residual/assay-discordant, or sign-constrained hidden synergy.

Main message: **every failure mode is diagnostically interpretable**.
