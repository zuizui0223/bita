# Figure captions — identification-design manuscript

**Figure 1. A total attraction-by-defence interaction defines an identified set rather than a unique mechanism.** A two-level attraction (`A`) by defence (`D`) factorial directly estimates `Delta_AD W = W11 - W10 - W01 + W00`. The alternative allocations shown are examples from the set of channel combinations compatible with the same total interaction. Biological bounds or selective interventions shrink that set; the total interaction alone does not select one allocation.

**Figure 2. Crossed consumer interventions identify channel contrasts and test the separability assumption.** The proposed design crosses the same two-level `A × D` manipulation with antagonist and pollinator access, yielding 16 cells. Antagonist-exclusion contrasts estimate attraction-dependent antagonist relief (`rho_delta`); pollinator-presence contrasts estimate the pollinator-dependent interference increment (`iota_increment_delta`), which requires the pollinator-absent `A×D` baseline (`m0_delta`) to recover total `iota_delta`. Dependence of either contrast on the other consumer state is the same `A×D×G×P` four-way interaction up to sign, so a non-zero four-way term rejects the simple separable channel representation.

**Figure 3. Joint cost is independently assayed rather than defined by subtraction.** The crossed consumer experiment leaves an unallocated joint channel `U_delta = rho_delta - iota_delta - Delta_AD W`; this residual is not automatically joint cost. A separate `A×D` assay under standardized or suppressed biotic pathways estimates `kappa_assay_delta`. Agreement supports the intended allocation; disagreement diagnoses an omitted pathway, non-selective intervention, baseline misspecification, or scale mismatch. If `Delta_AD W > 0` while `rho_delta <= iota_delta`, the remaining joint channel must be negative, but assigning that sign specifically to `kappa` still requires the independent assay.

**Figure 4. Constituent channels and complementary experimental faces recur, but mechanism allocation remains unidentified.** The retained route synthesis contains 56 records across 25 independent biological clusters and covers all four marginal pathway families; these overlapping counts demonstrate recurrence rather than prevalence or channel identification. The upper panel shows complementary experimental faces: Kessler et al. (2008) supplies an A×D-like trait factorial, Egan et al. (2021) a consumer-factorial backbone with measured traits, and Theis and Adler (2012) an A×G×pollination-supplementation bridge. Supplemental hand pollination is not a pollinator-access toggle. The lower panel shows the *Impatiens capensis* retrofit, where all eight target 95% intervals cross zero. Across 17 screened high-information systems, independent joint-cost assays and full channel identification remain absent; the stronger result is design fragmentation across the target A×D×G×P hypercube.

**Figure 5. Experimental roadmap from interaction detection to mechanism identification.** An identifiable study begins with independently manipulable attraction and flower-specific defence coordinates, then selects a system permitting selective antagonist and pollinator interventions. The `A×D×G×P` experiment estimates total and consumer-channel contrasts; the pollinator-absent baseline is measured; the four-way contrast tests separability; and an independent cost assay constrains the remaining joint channel. Separability failure or assay disagreement remains informative because it identifies which causal representation is inadequate.
