# Figure plan — integrated trait-differentiation Chapter 2

The final article should use **five Main figures**. The two new architecture figures lead; the mature BITA identification figures are compressed/reused behind them rather than appended wholesale.

## Figure 1 — Balance versus differentiation

Source: `trait_differentiation_figures/FIGURE_1_BALANCE_TO_DIFFERENTIATION.svg`

Purpose:

- show the shared-axis compromise between two function-specific optima;
- show partial separation of two differentiated axes under residual coupling;
- introduce the decoupling fraction `s`;
- give the reader-facing identity `Delta_arch = s L_S* - K`.

This is the conceptual entry point and should be cited at the end of the first theory subsection.

## Figure 2 — Architecture boundary

Source: `trait_differentiation_figures/FIGURE_2_ARCHITECTURE_BOUNDARY.svg`

Purpose:

- plot the decision boundary `K = s L_S*`;
- show that stronger conflict load can pay for differentiation;
- show that stronger residual coupling lowers `s` and therefore lowers the cost ceiling under which differentiation pays;
- make clear that the same trade-off can resolve differently in systems with different decoupling capacity or architecture costs.
