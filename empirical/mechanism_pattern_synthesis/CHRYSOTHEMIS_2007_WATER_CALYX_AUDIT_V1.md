# Chrysothemis friedrichsthaliana 2007 water-calyx audit v1

## Source

Carlson JE, Harms KE. **The benefits of bathing buds: water calyces protect flowers from a microlepidopteran herbivore.** *Biology Letters* 3:405–407. DOI `10.1098/rsbl.2007.0095`.

The primary article is open access in PubMed Central (PMCID `PMC2390658`).

## Floral-D definition

The cup-like fused sepals hold liquid over developing buds for several weeks before anthesis.

```text
D = liquid-filled water calyx over developing floral buds
manipulation = unmanipulated water-filled calyx vs drained calyx vs drained-and-refilled control
antagonist = Alucitidae microlepidopteran floral herbivore
```

This is a direct manipulation of a flower-associated antagonist barrier.

## Source-verified Pattern

The moth oviposits into immature buds, and larvae subsequently consume developing anthers and stigmas. Across two flowering seasons the moth destroyed roughly one-quarter of per-plant flower production, exceeding losses from the remaining floral-herbivore guild combined.

Experimental draining more than doubled the odds that a flower became larva-infested and sterile relative to flowers retained under water-filled calyces:

```text
F_1,241 = 6.87
P = 0.009
odds ratio = 2.18
95% CI = 1.21–3.92
```

The primary abstract summarizes the manipulation as roughly halving egg deposition and subsequent herbivory when the liquid barrier is present.

Pattern state:

```text
D -> antagonism: protective
```

## Independence value

This is an independent evolutionary origin of a liquid floral barrier from *Pedicularis rex*:

```text
Chrysothemis:
  fused sepal water calyx protects developing buds from microlepidopteran oviposition

Pedicularis:
  cupulate water-holding bracts protect flowers/seeds from pre-dispersal seed herbivores
```

The two studies therefore strengthen recurrence of liquid physical barriers across unrelated plant lineages and antagonist attack modes.

## Pollinator boundary

The article notes that extensive video was collected for a separate pollination study, but it does not experimentally manipulate the water-calyx state and estimate legitimate pollinator response on the same flowers.

No `D -> pollination` record is therefore created.
