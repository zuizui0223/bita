## Stable empirical rule

The strongest repeated rule is an **effective-domain separation principle**.

A defence/access trait can reduce antagonist costs without suppressing legitimate pollination when antagonists and pollinators do not experience the same effective defence domain. Separation can arise through:

```text
consumer susceptibility
chemical dose
cumulative exposure
tissue or attack route
body-size/access geometry
space on the flower
time/developmental stage
visitor functional mode
response stage
```

If the domains overlap, pollinator interference can appear. If antagonists bypass the domain, the focal defence channel can fail. These states recur across chemical and physical implementations and survive explicit boundary cases.

## U7 is no longer a pure design gap

### Impatiens

The existing strict coefficient-level cluster remains sign-unresolved: reproductive-component interaction point estimates differ in sign and their confidence intervals include zero.

### Kessler et al. 2008

A four-genotype Nicotiana field experiment crossed BA attraction with nicotine state. Published female outcross capsule-set cells imply a positive probability-scale interaction of roughly +0.19 to +0.25; the corresponding logit interaction remains positive over the reported rounded low-state range. This is a sign-robust direct-factorial signal but remains lower-specificity because nicotine silencing is systemic and exact day-by-genotype denominators were not recovered.

### Kessler et al. 2015

A higher-specificity RNAi experiment independently crossed floral BA scent with floral nectar production. Orienting `D` as nectar restriction is biologically admissible because nectar absence reduces Manduca sexta oviposition, so the restriction has an antagonist-reduction role.

Using source-reported normalized seed means:

```text
native visitor community: Delta_AD = -0.7900
Manduca sexta:            Delta_AD = -0.4320
Hyles lineata:            Delta_AD = +0.8699
```

The same crossed floral A/D architecture therefore changes sign with pollinator context. This is a direct factorial realization of the theory's core point: **the sign is conditional on ecological channel balance rather than universal**.

The source reports omnibus Friedman tests and pairwise comparisons but not a dedicated A-by-D interaction coefficient. Replicate-level numerical source data were not recovered after targeted eLife, PMC, MPG.PuRe, Bio-protocol, and repository searches. Therefore the sign reversal of the published factorial means is identified, while formal interaction uncertainty remains unidentified.

## Interpretation of U7

The direct evidence now agrees with the broader marginal-route architecture:

> context does not merely change the magnitude of one fixed attraction-defence sign; it can reverse the sign of the crossed floral factorial itself.

This materially strengthens the Mechanism -> Pattern structure. The empirical literature no longer only supports the theory through constituent routes. At least one high-specificity crossed floral factorial exhibits the predicted kind of contextual sign switching at the level of the joint trait architecture.

What it does **not** do is identify the channel decomposition `rho`, `iota`, and `kappa`, because total factorial outcomes remain compatible with multiple channel allocations under Proposition 1.

## Current strongest synthesis

> Floral attraction repeatedly exposes flowers to both mutualists and antagonists across sensory modalities. Flower-specific defence/access limitation repeatedly reduces antagonist use through chemically and physically distinct mechanisms. The realised attraction-defence relationship depends on whether pollinators and antagonists occupy separable effective response/access domains. This switching architecture recurs across marginal routes, matched defence systems, threshold and geometry transitions, and now a direct crossed floral factorial in which the discrete A-by-D sign reverses across pollinator contexts. The repeatable object is therefore the mechanism-level sign-switching architecture, not one universal attraction-defence sign.
