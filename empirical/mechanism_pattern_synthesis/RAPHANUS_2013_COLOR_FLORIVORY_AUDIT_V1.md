# Raphanus sativus 2013 color–florivory audit v1

## Source

McCall AC, Murphy SJ, Venner C, Brown M. **Florivores prefer white versus pink petal color morphs in wild radish, Raphanus sativus.** *Oecologia* 172:189–195. DOI `10.1007/s00442-012-2480-z`.

## Pattern question

Does a non-scent floral attraction/display axis also structure antagonist use?

Here the focal axis is petal color morph, a canonical visual floral signal. The study directly tests florivore damage and preference across white versus pink flowers.

## Source-verified Pattern

The study reports:

```text
8 field populations
4 years
white-flowered plants: greater floral damage than pink-flowered plants
```

Experimental florivore assays independently support the field pattern:

- both generalist and Brassicaceae-specialist florivores preferred white petals in choice and short-term no-choice tests;
- generalists gained more weight on white than pink petals;
- specialists had similar weight gain on the two morphs.

Thus the visual floral color axis changes antagonist use, while consumer specialization changes whether color-associated preference is accompanied by differential performance.

## Theory-facing mapping

Admitted:

```text
A(color) -> floral antagonism
consumer-specialization dependence of downstream performance
visual-signal replication outside scent systems
```

The study adds a distinct visual-signal lineage to the Pattern architecture.

## Pollinator relation

Earlier independent work in wild radish demonstrates pollinator discrimination among color morphs, including pollinator-taxon and seasonal differences in color preference. That literature supports the biological interpretation of petal color as an attraction/display axis, but it is not pooled with the 2013 florivory study as a same-unit or same-study multi-route estimate.

Therefore:

```text
Raphanus color is a justified A axis
2013 study contributes A -> antagonism
1987/other pollinator studies do not turn this into same-system A -> pollination evidence
```

## Inference boundary

Not admitted:

```text
color difference = universal A -> antagonism sign
white preference by florivores = pollinator preference
florivore preference = W_AD
consumer performance difference = defence D
```

The generalist/specialist performance contrast is retained as a consumer-context Pattern, not as a separate defence route.
