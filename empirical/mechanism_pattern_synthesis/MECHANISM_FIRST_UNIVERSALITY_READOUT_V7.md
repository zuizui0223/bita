## U7 is no longer an empty design cell

### Kessler 2015: cleaner strict floral factorial

Kessler et al. 2015 independently silenced:

```text
A = floral benzylacetone scent
D = floral reward/access restriction through loss of nectar secretion
```

in all four combinations.

The `D` orientation is not semantic relabelling: nectar absence directly reduced `Manduca sexta` oviposition, and the source verified the floral manipulation while finding no genotype differences in leaf volatile profiles, plant/flower morphology, or hand-pollinated seed production.

Using source-reported seed production normalized to EV, the direct discrete factorial contrast

```text
Delta_AD = W11 - W10 - W01 + W00
```

changes sign with pollinator context:

```text
native visitor community   -0.790
Manduca sexta              -0.432
Hyles lineata              +0.8699
```

The same A/D genotype architecture therefore produces substitution-compatible and complementarity-compatible states depending on the pollinator context.

This is much stronger theory-facing evidence than marginal-route recurrence because both traits are crossed in the same experiment and evaluated on the same reproductive outcome.

### Statistical boundary

The source used Friedman tests/post-hoc treatment comparisons rather than reporting a dedicated A-by-D interaction coefficient with uncertainty. The discrete signs above are arithmetic reconstructions from reported normalized means, not source-tested interaction coefficients.

Therefore U7 is **identified at the descriptive factorial-sign level**, but not yet as a pooled or local derivative estimate with uncertainty.

### Kessler 2008: independent positive sign-robust near-strict factorial

The earlier nicotine x benzylacetone field experiment also crossed all four states and produced a positive aggregate female-outcrossing factorial signal. Published aggregate constraints imply a positive probability-scale contrast (`+0.19` to `+0.25`) and a positive logit interaction over the rounded source range (`beta_AD ~ +1.02` to `+1.55`; interaction OR `~2.77` to `4.71`).

Its organ/intervention gate remains less clean because `Napmt1/2` silencing is systemic. Thus it is supporting direct-factorial replication, not the cleanest focal estimate.

## Why this changes the central Pattern conclusion

The empirical record now contains direct crossed floral evidence that the A x D sign can reverse **without changing the basic trait architecture**.

This means the strongest conclusion is no longer only:

> constituent routes recur, while the net sign remains unidentified.

A sharper conclusion is supported:

> direct attraction-by-antagonist-reducing floral trait interactions can occupy both complementarity-compatible and substitution-compatible states, and pollinator identity can switch the discrete interaction sign within one plant system.

That is exactly the type of state switching predicted by the mechanism-first decomposition.

## Stable cross-modal rule

The broader evidence remains consistent with an effective-domain separation rule:

- when antagonist and pollinator domains remain separable, antagonist relief can coexist with preserved pollination;
- when domains overlap, pollinator interference appears;
- when antagonists bypass the defence/access domain, D efficacy disappears.

Chemical dose/exposure, physical geometry, developmental timing, attack mode, consumer identity, and visitor functional mode all instantiate this same abstract switching architecture.

Kessler 2015 adds a particularly strong **consumer-context switch on the direct A x D outcome itself**.

## U8 remains unresolved for a principled reason

No identified study isolates the direct cost-channel cross term `C_AD` from mutualist and antagonist effects. Generic allocation costs, pollinator deterrence, whole-plant resistance costs, or total reproductive A x D interactions cannot identify `kappa`.

Thus:

```text
strict C_AD estimates = 0
kappa = unidentified, not zero
U8 = field-design gap
```

## Current strongest synthesis

> Attraction signals repeatedly expose flowers to both mutualists and antagonists across sensory modalities. Antagonist-reducing floral traits repeatedly alter access through chemical, physical, spatial, temporal and reward-mediated routes. The sign generated by combining attraction and antagonist reduction is not universal: direct crossed floral evidence now shows both negative and positive discrete A x D states within the same plant system as pollinator context changes. The repeatable empirical object is therefore the mechanism-level switching architecture, not one universal sign.
