# Theory–empiricism boundary audit and Gate H adjudication v1

## Decision

**Gate H — theory/empiricism boundary: PASS for the current mechanism-pattern synthesis.**

The fixed theoretical object remains the local cross-curvature after the orientation gate,

```text
W_AD = rho - iota - kappa
```

and none of the empirical layers below is promoted to a direct estimate of that quantity. This audit fixes the allowed inferential boundary before manuscript reconstruction resumes.

## H1 — no marginal-route synthesis is called an estimate of `W_AD`

**PASS.**

The four marginal families (`A_to_pollination`, `A_to_antagonism`, `D_to_antagonism`, `D_to_pollination`) are used to establish mechanism recurrence and conditionality only. The Leal larceny module is an environmental antagonist-pressure gate; the Sasidharan module is a trait-mediated cross-study response synthesis. Neither identifies the attraction-by-defence mixed partial.

Same-system multi-route evidence is kept distinct from the only strict direct `A x D` cluster.

## H2 — publication/study counts are not parameter estimates

**PASS.**

Counts such as 14 source-adjudicated study clusters, 10 same-system clusters, 11 sign-switch clusters, 32 Sasidharan study components, or 48 Leal female-fitness clusters are evidence-capacity/dependence descriptors only. They are not converted into `rho`, `iota`, `kappa`, `H`, `P`, or any other model parameter.

## H3 — finite-grid or screened-set proportions are not prevalence in nature

**PASS.**

The same-system regime counts and conditionality ontology explicitly describe recurrence in the current information-rich screened set. Sasidharan test-unit fractions are explicitly described as current-deposit assembled patterns. No such fraction is interpreted as the frequency of a regime, mechanism, floral volatile response, complementarity, or substitutability in nature.

## H4 — the direct `A x D` sign is not generalized beyond its study

**PASS.**

The strict direct layer contains one accepted cluster, `Gorden_Adler_2018_Impatiens_capensis`:

```text
CH fruits per plant per day: A x D = -0.0820 +/- 0.0548 SE
seeds per CH fruit:          A x D = +0.1040 +/- 0.1043 SE
```

Both confidence intervals include zero and the point signs differ across reproductive components. The project classifies this as **direct but unresolved**, not as evidence for a universal positive or negative attraction–defence interaction.

## H5 — no cross-outcome grand mean is the primary biological result

**PASS.**

Preference, visitation, consumption/residence, antagonist use, physiological detection, reproductive components, reward depletion, and fitness remain distinct outcome constructs. The mechanism-pattern synthesis uses a structured map of route recurrence, same-system architecture, and context switching. It does not average incompatible endpoints into one grand effect.

The Leal module reports its outcome strata separately. The Sasidharan module deliberately does not manufacture a common continuous effect across heterogeneous physiological and behavioral assays.

## Additional boundary checks fixed by the current evidence

### `kappa` is unidentified, not zero

The saturated direct joint-cost search found no strict simultaneous A+D intrinsic-cost estimate. This fixes the empirical state as **unidentified**. Marginal attraction costs, marginal defence costs, trait covariance, weak integration, or ecological interference cannot be used to manufacture `kappa`.

### Gate C does not identify the mixed partial

Two quantitative modules satisfy the empirical breadth requirement, but the combination of those modules is not an algebraic decomposition of `W_AD`. Module count is evidence architecture, not parameter identification.

### Same-system evidence is not direct interaction evidence

A study measuring `D_to_antagonism` and `D_to_pollination` in the same biological system is more informative than two unrelated marginal studies, but it still does not estimate `A x D` unless attraction and defence are linked and an interaction outcome is actually estimated.

### Conditionality recurrence is not prevalence

The five-class conditionality ontology records multiple ways in which mechanism state changes. It supports the claim that context dependence is empirically recurrent in the screened evidence base. It does not estimate how often any class occurs across flowering plants.

## Allowed integrated claims

The current empirical architecture supports the following bounded claims:

1. theory-relevant marginal mechanisms recur across multiple independent biological systems;
2. same-system evidence contains guarded, interference, shared-tracking, and context-switching architectures rather than one universal marginal sign;
3. direct attraction-by-defence estimation is rare in the registered search universe and the one strict current cluster is sign-unresolved;
4. conditionality recurs through multiple biological classes including trait expression, resource/exposure context, consumer identity, response stage/scale, and compound identity;
5. direct intrinsic joint A+D cost remains empirically unidentified under the saturated registered search;
6. two independent quantitative synthesis modules show that the empirical half is not carried by one literature alone.

## Prohibited integrated claims

Do not state that:

- the empirical synthesis estimates `W_AD`, `rho`, `iota`, or `kappa`;
- the empirical evidence proves attraction and defence are generally complementary or generally substitutable;
- the single `Impatiens` direct interaction determines the sign in other systems;
- regime or sign-switch counts are prevalence estimates;
- the Sasidharan florivore–pollinator contrast is a causal within-study role effect;
- the Leal larceny pooled effects generalize to all floral antagonists;
- absence of a direct joint-cost study implies `kappa = 0`.
