# Quantitative Pattern expansion search v1

## Question

After admitting Leal 2025 and Sasidharan 2023 as reproduced quantitative modules, does another large synthesis add a **distinct theory-facing Pattern axis** rather than merely more observations of an already represented class?

## Admitted / retained quantitative modules

### PM01 — Leal et al. 2025 floral larceny

Status: reproduced random-effects meta-analysis.

Distinct Pattern contribution:

```text
realised floral-antagonist cost
+ reward / legitimate-visitation / female-fitness response stages
+ extreme among-study heterogeneity
```

### PM02 — Sasidharan et al. 2023 floral volatiles

Status: reproduced quantitative cross-study synthesis.

Distinct Pattern contribution:

```text
shared floral-signal tracking
+ consumer-role / compound dependence
+ study-composition limitation
```

### PM03 — Haas-Desmarais et al. 2026 herbivory–pollination

DOI `10.1093/aob/mcaf258`.

Status: published multilevel meta-analysis; publisher supplementary package independently retrieved and hashed.

Published universe:

```text
171 studies
1,348 study cases
overall Hedges g ≈ -0.20 ± 0.04
I2 ≈ 71.4%
```

Distinct Pattern contribution:

```text
tissue / damage-mode dependence
natural versus simulated herbivory dependence
antagonist-context coupling to floral traits, pollinator attraction and reproduction
```

Boundary: herbivory is environmental antagonist pressure, not the focal floral defence trait `D`; no estimate is `rho`, `iota`, `kappa` or `W_AD`.

### PM04 — Caruso et al. 2019 agents of floral selection

DOI `10.1111/evo.13639`; Dryad `10.5061/dryad.2v8c5g0`.

Status: published selection meta-analysis retained as secondary context; Dryad landing/API metadata and workbook identities verified, but current file-byte routes are access-limited.

Published main analysis:

```text
755 directional selection gradients with uncertainty
36 articles
487 paired treatment contrasts
```

Distinct Pattern contribution:

```text
selection-agent dependence
floral-trait-class dependence
pollinator-guild dependence
```

Boundary: directional selection gradients are not `W_AD` and other-biotic manipulation is not automatically antagonist pressure `H`.

### PM05 — Junker & Blüthgen 2010 floral scent

DOI `10.1093/aob/mcq045`.

Status: cross-synthesis replication candidate.

Published universe:

```text
18 publications
425 observations
```

Distinct Pattern contribution:

```text
consumer dependency / functional-group filtering of floral-scent responses
```

Boundary: obligate flower visitors are not deterministically pollinators, and facultative visitors are not deterministically antagonists.

## Additional search outcome

Targeted expansion searches were run around:

- toxic nectar / nectar secondary metabolites;
- pollinator-guild tolerance of floral chemicals;
- ant–pollinator conflict in ant-plants;
- flower-surface physical defences;
- nursery pollination / seed predation;
- pollination-syndrome × florivory;
- floral colour × florivory;
- nectar robbery × pollinator response.

These searches yielded valuable **source-level route and conditionality systems**, which were admitted separately, but no sixth quantitative synthesis was found that added a theory-facing Pattern dimension not already represented by PM01–PM05.

In particular, further nectar-toxicity syntheses/studies map onto already represented:

```text
consumer role / guild dependence
compound identity / dose dependence
guarded chemical defence
```

and further herbivory/pollination literature maps onto already represented:

```text
tissue / damage-mode context
response-stage dependence
pollinator suppression / null states
```

## Quantitative stopping decision

```text
distinct quantitative Pattern axes currently represented: 5
new distinct axis found after PM03–PM05 search expansion: no
raw study/case counts pooled across modules: prohibited
```

**Decision: QUANTITATIVE_PATTERN_AXIS_SEARCH_SATURATED_FOR_CURRENT_MANUSCRIPT.**

This does not claim that no additional meta-analysis exists. It means further identified syntheses currently duplicate an admitted Pattern class rather than changing the manuscript's mechanism-facing Pattern architecture.
