# Mechanism-pattern synthesis completion gate v1

## Status

This gate replaces the old idea that manuscript work should resume only after one `D_to_pollination` stratum reaches a fixed study count.

The manuscript remains frozen until the empirical evidence architecture is sufficiently covered to support an integrated theory + synthesis paper.

## Why the gate changes

The theory is conditional because several mechanisms jointly determine the local attraction-defence relationship. A single pooled route cannot validate the mixed partial and should not be treated as the sole empirical bottleneck.

Completion is therefore judged by **mechanism coverage, source quality, and direct-interaction search saturation**, not by one universal `k >= 5` rule.

## Required gates

### Gate A — direct interaction search completed

A dedicated search for studies measuring or manipulating attraction and defence jointly must be completed and source-adjudicated.

Pass condition:

```text
all registered direct_AxD search families screened to the declared stop rule
all plausible Tier 1 candidates full-text checked where accessible
direct effects extracted when recoverable
negative result allowed: zero eligible studies is a publishable evidence gap if search saturation is documented
```

### Gate B — all four marginal mechanism families have an explicit empirical state

Each route must be classified as one of:

```text
quantitatively synthesized
quantitatively sparse but source-verified
directionally synthesized
source-verified evidence gap
```

Routes:

```text
A_to_pollination
A_to_antagonism
D_to_antagonism
D_to_pollination
```

No route may remain merely an abstract-level candidate pool in the integrated Results.

### Gate C — at least two independent quantitative mechanism modules

At least two biologically distinct theory-relevant modules must have source-audited numerical effects from independent studies or a defensible reanalysis of a deposited synthesis.

The current antagonist-pressure/larceny module can satisfy one module after integration and audit. A second module must come from a trait-mediated route or direct interaction layer.

This prevents one literature from carrying the entire empirical half of the paper.

### Gate D — sign-switch analysis executed where the data permit

For any route containing plausible opposite directions across doses or contexts, the synthesis must preserve those contrasts and explicitly test or map conditionality.

Priority contexts:

```text
natural versus supra-natural dose
reward quality / alternative resource context
consumer identity or specialization
mechanism class
environmental P/H context
```

A universal mean may be reported only as a subordinate descriptive quantity when large heterogeneity or sign switching is present.

### Gate E — same-system multi-route evidence audited

Studies that measure multiple routes in the same biological system must be identified separately from unrelated marginal studies.

Pass condition:

```text
same-system linkage is explicitly coded
shared study panels are not counted as independent across routes
co-occurring guarded-attraction and pollinator-interference evidence is summarized
```

### Gate F — direct joint-cost evidence state fixed

The direct joint-cost term must have a transparent empirical state.

Pass condition:

```text
dedicated matched A+D cost/allocation search completed
eligible evidence extracted if found
or a documented evidence gap is reported
```

Separate attraction and defence studies must not be used to manufacture a joint-cost estimate.

### Gate G — synthesis-level bias and robustness audit completed

For every quantitative module with enough studies:

```text
study independence audited
influence / leave-one-cluster-out analysis run
heterogeneity reported
small-study or publication-bias diagnostics used only where appropriate to the metric and k
metric-specific limitations stated
```

Directional maps must report source verification level and coding uncertainty.

### Gate H — theory/empiricism boundary validated

The integrated readout must pass the following claim checks:

```text
no marginal-route synthesis is called an estimate of W_AD
no publication count is used as a parameter estimate
no finite-grid percentage is called prevalence in nature
no direct A x D sign is generalized beyond its study without synthesis support
no cross-outcome grand mean is presented as the primary biological result
```

## Manuscript-resumption decision

Resume manuscript reconstruction only when Gates A-H are satisfied.

The empirical paper may proceed even if some mechanisms are sparse, provided those gaps are the result of a documented, saturated search rather than unfinished screening. The intended endpoint is a map of what is general, what is context dependent, and what remains empirically unidentified.
