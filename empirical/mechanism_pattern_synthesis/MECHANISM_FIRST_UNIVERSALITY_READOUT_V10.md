# Mechanism-first universality readout v10

## Resolution

The earlier effective-domain separation / selectivity rule is now resolved as a **one-sided necessary window**, not a sufficient criterion for attraction-defence complementarity.

For the deployed family,

```text
W_AD = antagonist relief - pollinator interference - joint-cost curvature.
```

With non-negative joint-cost curvature,

```text
W_AD > 0  =>  antagonist relief > pollinator interference.
```

Across the declared 2,592 evaluations, there are zero complementary points outside that window. Window precision is 77.2%, so about 23% of in-window evaluations remain substitutable. With joint cost set to zero, the window and sign criterion coincide exactly.

The empirical meaning of the earlier route-separation result is therefore narrower and stronger: **domain separation identifies where complementarity can occur; it does not determine that complementarity occurs.**

## Empirical gate

The one-sided bound can fail only if joint-cost curvature is negative, and only when sufficiently negative. The strict evidence layer contains no direct `c_AD` estimate. The sign of `c_AD` is therefore the minimal empirical gate for the biological applicability of the theorem.

A first test is a 2 x 2 allocation design comparing neither trait, attraction only, defence only, and both. This can test the sign of joint-cost curvature without pollinators, antagonists, or a fitness assay. A full `A x D` factorial with channel-specific outcomes and total fitness remains necessary to estimate `W_AD` itself.

## What Part II establishes

The saturated 25-system Pattern synthesis still supports recurrent constituent mechanisms and recurrent context-dependent switching. Guarded defence, spatial/temporal/attack-mode filtering, visitor functional-mode routing, lifecycle-stage role reversal, and response/population/trait-class dependence remain admitted Pattern classes.

The floral-larceny synthesis supplies one measured exposure path. Female reproductive success has pooled LRR -0.210 across 48 independent clusters; 35/48 clusters are negative, but the 95% prediction interval is -1.13 to +0.71. Leave-one-cluster-out and declared ingest sensitivities preserve the pooled direction, while six declared moderator analyses explain only 0-8% of the heterogeneity.

Thus the antagonist-pressure gate is open on average, but its realised magnitude and even sign are system dependent. The declared context axes do not yet locate that variation. This is compatible with a moving selectivity window, but it does not predict the window location in a new system.

The mechanism chain is not claimed. Only five clusters measure nectar, visitation, and female fitness together; two show all three negative. Among eleven clusters measuring both nectar and visitation, the within-study association is r = -0.17. The result is constituent-path evidence, not an end-to-end demonstrated reward-mediated mechanism.

## Integrated claim

The complete story is hierarchical:

1. constituent mechanisms recur;
2. selectivity / route separation defines a necessary window under non-negative joint cost;
3. exposure varies strongly among systems and moves that window;
4. the unmeasured sign of `c_AD` decides whether the strongest one-sided boundary applies biologically;
5. total `W_AD` remains a harder factorial measurement.

The paper should not claim a universal positive or negative `W_AD`, a two-sided universal selectivity criterion, a demonstrated reward-mediated larceny chain, or an empirical estimate of `c_AD`.

The defensible integrated claim is:

> Floral attraction-defence complementarity is bounded by a one-sided selectivity window under non-negative joint cost. Nature repeatedly implements the constituent pathways and context-dependent switching needed to move that window, while the exposure term is demonstrably heterogeneous and the joint-cost sign that decides the bound's biological applicability remains unmeasured.

This closes the current story at a falsifiable boundary. The remaining unknowns are explicit next experiments rather than unresolved logical links inside the claim.
