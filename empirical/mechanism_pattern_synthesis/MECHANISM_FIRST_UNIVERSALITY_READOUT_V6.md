## Stable cross-modal rule

The strongest repeatable empirical object remains an **effective-domain separation rule**.

Selective/complementarity-compatible states occur when the antagonist and pollinator channels do not experience the focal defence identically. The separating coordinate can be:

```text
consumer susceptibility
chemical dose
cumulative exposure
tissue or attack route
body-size/access geometry
space on the flower
time/developmental stage
visitor functional mode
response stage
```

When the domains overlap, pollinator interference appears. When antagonists bypass the domain, the focal D channel fails. This three-state logic is reproduced in chemically and physically distinct systems and survives explicit boundary/falsification cases.

## New U7 result: Kessler et al. 2008 is a genuine direct-factorial candidate

Kessler et al. 2008 (`10.1126/science.1160072`) independently blocked:

```text
A candidate: floral benzylacetone emission
D candidate: nicotine / floral-nectar repellent state
```

in all four combinations in field-grown `Nicotiana attenuata`.

The source directly validates:

- BA as a floral attractant: BA-deficient plants receive fewer hummingbird/hawkmoth visits;
- nicotine as a floral deterrent/defence-like axis: nicotine reduces nectar removal, nectar robbing and florivory;
- shared reproductive outcomes: antherectomized female outcross capsule production and male seed siring.

Source-reported female outcross capsule production averaged approximately:

```text
A+,D+  EV     35%
A+,D-  PMT    12-14%
A-,D+  CHAL   12-14%
A-,D-  CP     12-14%
```

Thus the rounded descriptive factorial contrast

`W11 - W10 - W01 + W00`

is positive, approximately `+0.19` to `+0.25` over the reported low-state range; using 13% for each low state gives `+0.22`.

Male seed-siring success is also jointly highest in EV: EV sired 1.9x more seeds than CHAL, 2.2x more than PMT and 4.7x more than CP.

### Why U7 is not yet promoted to identified

The source closes more of the design gap than the previously catalogued ecological-agent factorials, but two strict issues remain:

1. **intervention specificity:** the measured focal phenotypes are floral, but `Napmt1/2` nicotine silencing is systemic and `Nachal1` is not clearly flower-exclusive. Non-floral effects therefore cannot yet be ruled out from plant-level fitness;
2. **interaction uncertainty:** the main article reports the four genotype cells and post-hoc comparisons, not a source-reported BA x nicotine interaction coefficient. Exact day/genotype data or supporting-material counts are needed for a formal discrete factorial interaction with uncertainty.

Current status:

```text
Kessler 2008 = DIRECT_AXD_HIGH_PRIORITY_CANDIDATE
not yet = clean local W_AD estimate
```

This materially changes the interpretation of the U7 gap: the field has at least one study architecture that nearly performs the desired trait factorial, rather than only ecological-agent near misses.

## U8 remains empty

Part I defines `kappa = C_AD`, the cross-curvature of the direct construction/allocation/physiological cost channel.

Kessler 2008 does not identify this channel because its reproductive outcomes combine mutualist service, antagonist effects and any direct costs. A positive or negative total A x D factorial contrast cannot isolate `C_AD`.

Likewise, studies of allocation trade-offs, defence-associated pollinator costs, correlated traits, or whole-plant resistance costs do not identify `kappa` unless they separately quantify the direct A-by-D cost channel on the declared coordinates.

Therefore:

```text

kappa state = unidentified, not zero
```

## Current strongest synthesis

> Floral attraction repeatedly increases discoverability to both mutualists and antagonists across sensory modalities. Flower-specific defence repeatedly lowers antagonist use through chemically and physically distinct mechanisms. Whether defence preserves pollination depends on separation of effective consumer domains; when that separation closes, pollinator interference appears, and when antagonists bypass it, defence efficacy disappears. This switching architecture is more repeatable than any single attraction-defence sign. Direct trait-factorial evidence is rare but not entirely absent: one new Nicotiana experiment approaches the required A x D design and yields a positive descriptive discrete interaction, although strict organ/intervention specificity and formal interaction uncertainty still need resolution.
