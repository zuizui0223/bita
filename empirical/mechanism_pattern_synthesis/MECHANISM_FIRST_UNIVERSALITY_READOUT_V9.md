# Mechanism-first universality readout v9

## Why v9 exists

A critical re-audit separated two questions that had begun to blur:

1. **Does a recurrent rule predict whether floral defence discriminates antagonists from pollinators?**
2. **Is the total attraction x defence fitness curvature `W_AD` empirically identified?**

The first now has substantial recurrent support. The second remains sparse and unresolved.

## U6 survived a circularity stress test

A concern with the phrase `effective-domain separation` is that it could become tautological if a system is called "separated" only after observing a preserved pollinator outcome.

A dedicated physical-system audit therefore coded geometry before invoking pollinator results.

The architecture-only rule was:

```text
SEPARATED  -> antagonist filtering can preserve legitimate function
OVERLAPPED -> antagonist filtering should impose pollination interference
BYPASSABLE -> the focal anti-antagonist D route should fail
```

Four particularly diagnostic systems matched those predictions:

```text
Codonopsis   SEPARATED  -> ant-filtering surface, pollinator foothold spared
Thunia       SEPARATED  -> robbery shortcut blocked, legitimate route preserved
Polemonium   OVERLAPPED -> narrower tube reduces ants but also pollen receipt / seed set
Salvia       BYPASSABLE -> robber bites exposed corolla; calyx shortening changes neither route
```

`Bejaria` provides an additional broad/overlapped boundary case: sticky petals/sepals strongly reduce florivory but can contact mutualistic insects as well, so guarded defence cannot be assumed.

This is still retrospective and small-N. It is not a prospective validation or prevalence estimate. But the rule is no longer merely a relabeling of observed signs: independently observable access geometry generates qualitative predictions.

## U7 was deliberately narrowed

### Kessler 2015

The RNAi study independently crosses floral benzylacetone emission and floral nectar production. If `D` is oriented as nectar restriction, the antagonist-reduction gate is supported because nectar reduction decreases `Manduca sexta` oviposition and genotype differences disappear in non-flowering plants.

However, the source-reported four-cell values used for the reconstructed sign reversal are pollinator-mediated outcross seed production from antherectomized flowers. They therefore represent a mutualist/pollination channel, not total fitness after adding antagonist losses and direct joint costs.

The source-mean crossed contrasts remain context heterogeneous:

```text
native visitor community  -0.790
Manduca sexta             -0.432
Hyles lineata             +0.8699
```

These values show that a direct floral trait factorial can change interaction sign across pollinator contexts in an `M_AD`-like channel. They must not be presented as total `W_AD` estimates.

### Kessler 2008

The earlier four-genotype study similarly provides direct trait-factorial reproductive-service evidence, but nicotine silencing is systemic and the outcome does not isolate the full channel balance. It therefore remains a lower-specificity channel-factorial candidate rather than total `W_AD` evidence.

### Impatiens

The existing `Impatiens capensis` reconstruction remains the cleaner total reproductive-outcome candidate. Its component interactions are estimable but their confidence intervals cross zero and point signs differ across reproductive components.

Thus total `W_AD` remains empirically unresolved.

## Strongest conclusion after re-audit

The evidence now supports a **hierarchical** conclusion rather than one headline claim at a single scale.

### Level 1 — recurrent constituent mechanisms

Attraction exposes flowers to mutualists and antagonists; antagonist pressure has recurrent costs; flower-specific defences can reduce antagonist access/use.

### Level 2 — recurrent switching rule

Whether defence is guarded, interfering, or ineffective is repeatedly associated with whether antagonist and pollinator interaction domains are separable, overlapping, or bypassable. This rule recurs across chemical susceptibility/exposure and physical route geometry.

### Level 3 — total attraction-defence curvature

The sign of total `W_AD` is **not yet empirically universal or well identified**. Channel-specific crossed interactions can change sign with consumer context, but the full `rho - iota - kappa` balance is rarely measured on compatible units.

## What is now clear

It is increasingly defensible to say:

> Nature repeatedly implements the constituent channels and the route-separation switching architecture predicted by the mechanism-first theory, but current experiments rarely measure enough channels together to identify the total attraction-defence curvature.

That is stronger than generic "context dependence" but narrower than claiming a universal sign or a fully validated total interaction.

## What would change the conclusion

Scientific work should continue until one of the following occurs:

1. multiple independent total-outcome `A x D` designs identify a stable sign rule or a reproducible context-driven sign switch;
2. channel-compatible experiments estimate `M_AD`, `G_AD`, and ideally `C_AD` sufficiently to reconstruct the total balance;
3. a strong counterexample breaks the domain-separation rule by showing a priori separated routes with systematic pollinator interference, or overlapped routes with robust guarded defence unexplained by another separator.

Until then:
