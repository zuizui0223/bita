# Erica 2021 corolla-stickiness audit v1

## Source

McCarren S, Coetzee A, Midgley J. **Corolla stickiness prevents nectar robbing in Erica.** *Journal of Plant Research* 134:963–970. DOI `10.1007/s10265-021-01299-z`.

The primary abstract is indexed in PubMed.

## Pattern question

Does a flower-surface adhesive trait experimentally reduce floral antagonism in an independent lineage?

## Floral-D definition

The focal trait is corolla stickiness, quantified as adhesive strength and manipulated experimentally in flowers of the same species.

```text
D = corolla surface stickiness
antagonist outcome = nectar-robbing / floral damage incidence
```

The study also compares naturally sticky and non-sticky co-occurring Erica species and maps stickiness across the genus, but those comparative observations are not counted as additional independent route clusters.

## Source-verified Pattern

The primary source reports that experimentally added stickiness reduced nectar robbing within species. Across co-occurring species, stickier flowers also had lower damage rates.

Across the genus, stickiness was most strongly associated with bird and long-proboscid-fly pollination syndromes and with floral morphologies predicted to face high robbing risk (narrow-mouthed and long corollas, shorter sepals).

Pattern state:

```text
D -> antagonism: protective
```

## Theory-facing interpretation

Admitted:

```text
flower-surface stickiness is an experimentally supported anti-robbing D
physical floral defence recurs independently of Bejaria stickiness chemistry and Pedicularis water barriers
D deployment is associated with floral/pollination architectures that create high-value or high-risk rewards
```

Not admitted:

```text
pollination-syndrome correlation = D -> pollination effect
bird/fly syndrome = focal attraction A
comparative genus association = direct A x D
stickiness effect = rho
```

## Independence boundary

The experiment and comparative analyses are one study cluster. Multiple Erica species do not become multiple independent route-level N in this synthesis.
