# Direct joint-cost (`kappa = C_AD`) targeted exhaustion v1

## Target

Part I defines total outcome as

`W = M - G - C`

and the direct joint-cost term as

`kappa = C_AD`.

The empirical target is therefore **not** a generic cost of defence, not a pollinator cost of defence, and not a negative association between attraction and defence. It is the cross-curvature / interaction of the same focal `A` and `D` axes in a direct construction, allocation, or physiological cost channel that is separable from mutualist service and antagonist loss.

## Targeted source classes audited

The final search explicitly targeted the following classes:

1. floral display or scent x chemical defence allocation experiments;
2. floral attraction x flower-specific defence factorial manipulations under consumer exclusion;
3. resistance-cost experiments measuring reproductive traits;
4. defence x pollination x herbivory factorials;
5. metabolic/integration studies linking floral attractive chemistry and defence chemistry;
6. transgenic floral chemistry studies with two independently varied traits.

## Closest sources and why they fail `C_AD`

### Kessler et al. 2008 — Nicotiana

BA and nicotine are independently varied in four genotype states and shared reproductive outcomes are measured. This is a high-priority total A x D candidate, but the outcome integrates pollination, florivory/robbing and any direct physiological costs. It does not isolate a direct cost channel `C`.

### Santangelo et al. 2019 — Trifolium

Cyanogenic defence is crossed with herbivore suppression and pollination environment, and defence alters selection on reproductive traits. However the attraction trait is not independently manipulated, HCN is not a flower-specific D axis, and allocation cost is not separated as an A-by-D direct cost surface.

### Strauss et al. 1999 — Brassica resistance cost in pollination currency

High/low resistance lines are used to test pollinator-mediated ecological costs of resistance. Such a pollination-mediated cost belongs to the mutualist channel, not automatically to direct cost `C_AD`, and the focal A axis is not independently varied.

### Arabis alpina chemical integration study

Floral scent and foliar defence compounds are measured for phenotypic integration. Correlation / lack of integration does not identify direct joint cost curvature.

### Brassica herbivory / floral signalling studies

Herbivory can alter floral scent and pollinator attraction, consistent with resource or signalling trade-offs. But herbivore induction is context `H`, not independently manipulated floral D, and the resulting fitness effects do not isolate `C_AD`.

## Result

No audited source supplies all of:

```text
validated focal floral A
validated focal flower-specific D
independent variation/manipulation of A and D
a direct cost outcome separable from M and G
an explicit A x D interaction / cross-curvature on that cost outcome
```

Therefore:

```text
strict direct C_AD estimates: 0
kappa status: UNIDENTIFIED / FIELD-DESIGN GAP
```

## Interpretation

This is an identification result, not evidence that joint intrinsic costs are absent.

The literature contains many plausible cost mechanisms — allocation trade-offs, systemic defence costs, pollinator-mediated costs, biosynthetic integration, and induced changes in reproductive signalling — but none located in this targeted pass isolates the theoretical `C_AD` channel on the focal floral coordinates.

## Stop rule for U8

Do not continue broad searching for generic defence costs. Reopen U8 only if a source is found that independently varies a validated floral attraction trait and flower-specific defence trait while measuring a direct construction/allocation/physiological cost outcome under conditions that separate that cost from mutualist and antagonist channels.

Until then:

`kappa remains unidentified, not zero.`
