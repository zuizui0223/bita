# Mechanism-first universality readout v3

## Scientific frame

This project proceeds **Mechanism -> predicted Pattern -> replication/universality test**.

The empirical task is not to discover a pattern and then invent a mechanism. Part I fixes the mechanism classes and switching logic first; Part II asks whether those predicted constituent routes and state transitions recur independently in nature.

## The empirical rule is now sharper than context dependence

The evidence supports a candidate **selectivity-window rule**:

```text
antagonist response threshold < realised defence exposure < pollinator interference threshold
```

When realised floral defence lies in this window, antagonist relief can be gained while legitimate pollination is preserved or functionally routed. When dose or cumulative exposure crosses the pollinator threshold, the same defence can lose selectivity and become pollinator-interfering.

The apparent state also depends on response stage: arrival frequency, residence time, reward removal, legitimate handling, pollen transfer, reproduction, and antagonist oviposition/use can show different signs under the same floral phenotype.

## Independent replication architecture

### Selective/guarded state — matched quantitative anchors

1. **Catalpa speciosa** — chemical consumer selectivity. Strong suppression of potential-thief consumption with approximately unchanged legitimate-pollinator reward consumption.
2. **Pedicularis rex** — physical attack-mode selectivity. Seed predation strongly reduced while robber and legitimate-pollinator visitation are null-compatible; robbers can bypass the water barrier.
3. **Thunia alba** — physical functional-mode selectivity. Robbery strongly reduced, arrival approximately unchanged, legitimate pollination and fruit set strongly increased; the same Bombus shifts ecological function.

These are independent taxa and distinct defence implementations. The class is therefore not a single-compound anecdote.

### Window closure / state switching — independent replications

1. **Polemonium viscosum** — moderate 2PE guarded/null-cost state; high 2PE pollinator interference.
2. **Asclepias spp.** — single-bout bee response null at high natural cardenolide concentrations; colony deterrence emerges over days; monarch flower foraging and oviposition also separate by response stage.
3. **Nicotiana attenuata** — repellents reduce ant visitation and pollinator handling/consumption but can increase visit number; sign changes by pollinator response stage.
4. **Gelsemium sempervirens** — high gelsemine broadly deters robbers and most pollinators; lower treatment responses are more species-specific.
5. **Aconitum lycoctonum** — antagonist suppression occurs at lower chemical exposure than strong legitimate-pollinator interference, supporting separated consumer thresholds.

These systems independently support the idea that selectivity is a **dynamic exposure-dependent state**, not a fixed binary property of a defence trait.

### Falsification / boundary cases

- **Lupinus argenteus pollen alkaloids**: defence-like floral chemistry does not automatically create an effective focal D route; pollen-thief deterrence is absent and microbial interpretation remains incomplete. This prevents coding chemistry as defence merely from compound identity.
- **Gelsemium high-dose state**: strong defence can impose pollinator costs. Guarded defence is therefore not universal.

## New targeted direct-design audit

Several studies are unusually close to the theory-facing design but fail the strict trait-level gate:

- **Helleborus foetidus**: factorial pollinator x herbivore manipulation demonstrates nonadditive fitness effects, but manipulates ecological interactions rather than floral A and D traits.
- **Fragaria vesca**: factorial herbivory x pollination experiment demonstrates diffuse/context-dependent selection on attraction and defence-related traits, but does not estimate an A:D trait interaction.
- **Cucurbita volatile factorial assays**: directly demonstrate shared versus consumer-specific signal tracking, but do not cross a focal attraction trait with a separately validated defensive trait on plant fitness.
- **Petunia floral blend decomposition**: demonstrates separable attractive and defensive components within floral scent, but lacks the crossed A x D fitness design.
- **Nicotiana benzylacetone**: one compound can be both pollinator-attracting and florivore-deterring; this is dual function/pleiotropy, not two independently varied trait axes.

Thus the U7/U8 gap is increasingly interpretable as a **field design gap**, not simply failure to search broadly enough.

## What can now be claimed

Supported:

> Across independent floral systems, antagonist-reducing mechanisms repeatedly coexist with conditional pollinator effects. Selective defence states recur across consumer, attack-mode and functional-mode filters, while independent chemical systems show that those states can close with dose, cumulative exposure or response stage. The repeatable object is therefore not one universal sign, but a mechanism-level switching rule.

Not yet supported:

> A universal positive or negative `W_AD`.

Also not supported:

> A universal numeric threshold ratio, a universal zero pollinator cost, or `kappa = 0`.

5. Attempt a formal meta-regression only if a compatible matched outcome lane reaches sufficient independent systems. Do not manufacture a common scale across visitation, handling, predation, oviposition and reproductive outcomes.
6. If those cells remain absent after targeted source exhaustion, promote the **missing-design matrix itself** as a result and keep U7/U8 unidentified.
