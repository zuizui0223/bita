# Floral defence selectivity — Milestone 1 readout

## Status

Milestone 1 has migrated the existing matched-defence evidence into a new macro-analysis layer **without replacing any previous BITA result**.

Current analysis-ready registry:

```text
14 independent matched-system clusters
Stage 1 eligible: 13
Stage 2 strict: 2
Stage 2 null-compatible: 3
Stage 2 transition: 4
hold-out clusters migrated: 0
```

The remaining matched systems include pollinator-outcome information that is unresolved for the strict Stage-2 comparison, transition states, or boundary cases. The original same-system D-side set is now fully inherited, including Ipomopsis and the unresolved Impatiens panel.

## What was preserved

The new module is a re-indexing and analysis layer over existing evidence. It preserves, rather than supersedes:

- the source-adjudicated synthesis of **56 directional route records from 25 independent biological clusters** and the 17-system high-information frontier;
- the direct and near-direct `A x D` work, including Kessler interaction bounds and partial-identification results;
- the mechanism-identification ladder and crossed-intervention logic;
- the larceny quantitative synthesis and other route-level quantitative lanes;
- the outcome-blind effective-domain coding and falsification work;
- the matched quantitative/source-model anchors for Catalpa, Pedicularis, and Thunia;
- the registered hold-out domain-rule validation protocol;
- the existing manuscript and submission package, which are not rewritten in this milestone.

## Quantitative anchors retained

Three already-audited systems provide the strongest matched numerical starting points:

- **Catalpa speciosa** — strong antagonist suppression with a legitimate-pollinator consumption estimate near zero;
- **Pedicularis rex** — strong seed-predator suppression, robber bypass, and null-compatible pollinator visitation;
- **Thunia alba** — strong robbery suppression, unchanged visitor arrival, and increased pollinia transfer / fruit set.

Their different outcome metrics remain on their source-supported scales. They are not collapsed into a synthetic universal selectivity effect.

## What the current corpus can and cannot do

Stage 1 is already well populated for asking whether a proposed flower-associated defence actually affects its antagonist route.

Stage 2 is not yet sufficiently populated for a high-dimensional cross-system model of pollinator preservation. The current strict binary lane contains only two independent clusters, with three additional null-compatible systems and four transition systems in which dose, exposure duration, or response stage changes the pollinator state. Impatiens remains outside the strict Stage-1 efficacy gate because its same-panel D-to-antagonism sign is unresolved.

Therefore Milestone 1 **does not yet establish** that effective-domain separation predicts pollinator preservation across floral systems. It establishes a reproducible evidence base and identifies exactly which missing matched contrasts control that test.

## Next scientific step

Do not broaden back into generic floral-trait searching. The next expansion is a focused systematic search for same-system matched-D studies that add one of the missing Stage-2 cells:

1. effective antagonist reduction + direct pollinator preservation/improvement;
2. effective antagonist reduction + direct pollinator impairment;
3. outcome-blind overlap/separation architecture with a direct pollinator outcome;
4. independent hold-out systems under the already registered 2020–2026 protocol.

Only after that expansion should the domain-separation versus chemical/physical model comparison be promoted to the main ecological result.
