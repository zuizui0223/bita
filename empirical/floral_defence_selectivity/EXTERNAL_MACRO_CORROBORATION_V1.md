# External macro corroboration v1

These studies are useful because they test broader access/resistance-routing consequences but do not satisfy the strict same-D matched-defence gate.

They are **not added to matched-D N**.

## Leonard et al. 2013 — experimental route choice

Citation:

Leonard AS, Brent J, Papaj DR, Dornhaus A (2013) Floral Nectar Guide Patterns Discourage Nectar Robbing by Bumble Bees. *PLoS ONE* 8:e55914. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0055914.

Design:

- artificial flowers;
- the same bumblebee species can visit legitimately or rob;
- nectar guide pattern manipulated;
- legitimate and robbery routes simultaneously available.

Primary source result:

- nectar guides made legitimate visits more efficient than robbing;
- nectar guides increased the relative frequency of legitimate visits.

BITA role:

> direct experimental corroboration that floral architecture can change **interaction route choice** rather than merely the total number of visits.

Boundary:

The nectar guide is an attraction/access signal, not an independently validated antagonist-reducing D trait, so this is not a primary matched-D replication.

## Adler, Leege & Irwin 2016 — geographic resistance/pollination mosaic

Citation:

Adler LS, Leege LM, Irwin RE (2016) Geographic variation in resistance to nectar robbing and consequences for pollination. *American Journal of Botany* 103:1819–1828. DOI 10.3732/ajb.1600114.

Design:

- common gardens in northern and southern Georgia;
- Gelsemium sempervirens genotypes from both regions;
- robbing, pollinator attraction, floral/plant traits and reproduction measured.

Primary source result:

- northern genotypes were about half as likely to be nectar-robbed;
- the same northern genotypes received about half as many pollinator visits;
- plant-level traits were more strongly associated with resistance than floral size, shape or chemistry.

BITA role:

> independent population/geographic evidence that resistance to floral exploitation can covary with reduced pollinator attraction.

Boundary:

The study does not isolate one focal flower-specific D axis responsible for both effects. It therefore supports the broad conflict architecture but does not enter the matched-D state-recovery analysis.

## Why these external systems matter

Together with the Sakhalkar network reanalysis, they show that the active BITA principle is not confined to one evidence format:

```text
matched same-D systems
    -> state recovery

within-system exposure shifts
    -> state switching

Sakhalkar multispecies network
    -> geometry predicts robbing vs thieving

Leonard experimental artificial flowers
    -> floral pattern changes legitimate vs robbery route

Adler geographic common gardens
    -> resistance and pollinator attraction covary across genotypes
```

The external systems broaden ecological plausibility without inflating the strict matched-D sample.
