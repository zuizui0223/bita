## Validation conclusion

Post-rule evidence is not yet large enough for a conventional confirmatory meta-regression, but it is not empty:

1. one new matched system gives a direction-supported selective state;
2. one new experimental chemical system is compatible with selectivity without equivalence evidence;
3. one registered hold-out points in the predicted direction but remains statistically boundary-limited;
4. one independent community network gives a significant species-level access-routing association.

Therefore the defensible statement is:

> **The effective-domain interpretation was generated from historical matched systems, and the limited evidence recovered afterward is directionally consistent across new matched systems, a hold-out system, and an independently assembled community network. Full confirmatory validation remains open.**

## Claim boundary

Do not report a post-rule success percentage or a binomial p-value: the evidence classes differ in design and strictness, and null-compatible results are not positive confirmations.
