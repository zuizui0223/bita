# A-side signal-leakage macro readout v1

## Purpose

Recover the attraction-side biological content of legacy BITA without expanding the main defence-selectivity paper into a second full analysis.

The question is:

> when a floral attraction/display axis is measured against both pollinators and antagonists in the same study, is the signal mutualist-exclusive or does it also track antagonists?

## Evidence base

The legacy route ledger contains:

~~~text
A -> pollination unique study programs: 5
A -> antagonism unique study programs:  8
same-study paired A systems:             5
~~~

The five paired systems are recorded in `a_side_signal_leakage_registry.csv`.

## Paired states

~~~text
SHARED_TRACKING     3
ANTAGONIST_BIASED   1
UNRESOLVED          1
~~~

### Shared tracking

1. *Cucurbita* comparative sesquiterpenoid axis: higher floral sesquiterpenoids predict both higher specialist squash-bee visitation and higher cucumber-beetle floral use.
2. *Nicotiana attenuata* benzylacetone: floral scent increases *Manduca sexta* pollinator-mediated seed production and also increases *M. sexta* oviposition.
3. *Dalechampia scandens* showy bract area: larger bracts increase bee visitation/pollen receipt and seed predation.

### Antagonist-biased

*Cucurbita pepo* enhanced floral fragrance increases florivore attraction while no increase in pollinator attraction is detected in the same experiment.

### Unresolved

*Impatiens capensis* floral redness has a near-zero pollinator-visitation slope and a negative florivory point estimate, with uncertainty overlapping zero. It is not forced into a leakage state.

## Macro interpretation

Among the four paired systems with a directional ecological state, none supports a simple mutualist-exclusive attraction signal:

~~~text
shared mutualist + antagonist tracking: 3
antagonist-biased tracking:             1
mutualist-exclusive directional state:  0
~~~

This is not a prevalence estimate and is not a confirmatory 4/4 test. The studies were historically selected as informative route evidence.

The useful ecological conclusion is narrower:

> **floral attraction/display axes can be visible to antagonists as well as mutualists, so signal leakage is a recurrent constituent biology that creates demand for filtering or defence.**

## Relation to the refocused BITA paper

This lane should remain supplementary / Discussion-level unless new independent paired-A systems are added.

It complements, rather than competes with, the D-side effective-domain result:

~~~text
A-side: signals can leak to antagonists
D-side: access/exposure structure can filter antagonists from mutualists
network side: blocked access can reroute exploitation to bypass
~~~

This recovers a major piece of the original attraction–defence BITA logic without claiming that one A×D interaction identifies mechanism.

## Claim boundary

Do not claim:

- that 4/4 estimates population prevalence;
- that all floral signals attract antagonists;
- that Impatiens shows antagonist avoidance as a resolved state;
- that shared tracking proves the same sensory mechanism in pollinators and antagonists.
