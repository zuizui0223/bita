### Abstract

Flowers must attract mutualists while remaining exposed to florivores, nectar robbers, pathogens, and other antagonists. Attraction and flower-specific defence are therefore unlikely to form either a universal trade-off or a universal complementary syndrome. We develop a local diagnostic framework for one declared floral attraction trait, one declared flower-specific antagonist-reducing trait, and one declared fitness or biological-outcome scale. The focal quantity is the mixed partial of the outcome surface with respect to attraction and defence. After explicit orientation conditions are established, its sign is a balance among antagonist relief, interference with legitimate pollinators, and direct joint-cost curvature: `W_AD = rho - iota - kappa`. The same total curvature can be generated by different channel allocations, so total fitness alone does not identify mechanism. Endpoint-normalized sensitivity analysis of an implemented corollary produced both complementary and substitutable regimes across 2,592 declared evaluations; these are sensitivity diagnostics, not estimates of prevalence in nature. We then use a theory-driven mechanism-pattern synthesis to ask whether the constituent processes recur in empirical systems, under which contexts they change state, and which terms remain unidentified. The source-adjudicated architecture contains 38 route-level records across 14 independent biological study clusters, including ten same-system multi-route clusters and eleven independent context/sign-switch clusters. A dedicated direct `A x D` search saturated with one strict, sign-unresolved study, while a dedicated direct joint-cost search saturated with no strict estimate, leaving `kappa` unidentified rather than zero. Two quantitative deposited-synthesis modules independently show that floral antagonists can impose substantial reward, visitation, and female-fitness costs and that floral volatile responses are shared across pollinators and florivores but are strongly context dependent. These empirical results support the biological reality and conditionality of the constituent mechanisms, not an empirical calibration of `W_AD`. The combined framework therefore distinguishes what is theoretically decisive, what is empirically recurrent, and what still requires factorial measurement.
