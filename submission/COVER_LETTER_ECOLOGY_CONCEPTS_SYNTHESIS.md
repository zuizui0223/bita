# Cover letter — Ecology, Concepts & Synthesis

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Trait interaction is not ecological mechanism: an identification framework for multifunctional traits,”** for publication as a **Concepts & Synthesis** article in *Ecology*.

The manuscript addresses a recurring inference problem in ecology: two traits can interact strongly on fitness without the interaction identifying the biological pathway that generated it. Floral attraction and defence provide the detailed worked case, but the problem is general to multifunctional traits whose effects are transmitted through several ecological partners or physiological channels.

Our first contribution is to separate outcome claims that are often conflated. For a two-level attraction-by-defence factorial, the directly measurable interaction is `Delta_AD W = W11-W10-W01+W00`. A positive value establishes that defence makes the attraction effect more positive, but it does not necessarily show that attraction has changed from non-beneficial to beneficial. We therefore distinguish positive interaction relief from functional constraint release and strict negative-to-positive reversal.

Our second contribution is an explicit identification framework. Writing the total interaction as `Delta_AD W = rho_delta - iota_delta - kappa_delta` does not point-identify antagonist relief, pollinator interference and the remaining joint channel; it defines a set of compatible allocations. Biological restrictions or partial channel measurements can shrink this set. A selective `A × D × antagonist × pollinator` experiment can identify the two consumer-mediated channels when the trait contrasts remain invariant, the pollinator-absent baseline is handled explicitly, and the four-way interaction supports channel separability. The remaining joint channel requires an independent assay rather than being labelled from a residual.

The empirical synthesis shows why this distinction matters. The source-adjudicated route ledger contains **56 directional route records from 25 independent biological clusters**, demonstrating recurrence of all four constituent marginal pathway families. A stricter **17-system high-information audit** shows that trait factorials, consumer interventions and mechanistic measurements already exist, but largely in different studies. None of the screened systems closes the full channel-allocation design plus an independent remaining-channel assay. We therefore describe the empirical state as **recurrent constituent biology with fragmented identification**, not absence of relevant biology and not prevalence of a particular trait architecture.

The strongest direct system-level anchor is the manipulated floral benzylacetone-by-nicotine factorial of Kessler et al. (2008). Under registered aggregate constraints, the defended attraction effect is uniformly positive while the undefended attraction effect remains narrowly zero-compatible. This provides strong aggregate evidence for positive interaction relief while preserving the claim ceiling: strict release under source/design-based uncertainty and ecological channel allocation remain unresolved.

The manuscript is intentionally separated from our companion architecture-value framework. We do not claim novelty for the general idea that trade-offs can favor specialization, nor do we use a positive trait interaction as evidence that trait differentiation historically evolved. The companion SLK framework owns the transition from an identified shared-coordinate conflict budget through architecture value, accessibility, invasion, fixation and occupancy. BITA asks the orthogonal inference question that begins once multiple trait axes are observed: **which ecological mechanism generated their joint effect?**

We believe this fits *Ecology*'s Concepts & Synthesis section because the contribution is a reusable inference framework tied directly to experimental design. It turns a common verbal warning—interaction is not mechanism—into a sequence of estimands, identified sets, partial-identification bounds, selective interventions, separability diagnostics and independent assays, with a source-audited empirical map showing where existing studies stop along that sequence.

The scientific source has recently been refocused around this mechanism-identification thesis. The previously generated 30-page Main / 38-page Appendix package corresponded to an older integrated architecture-plus-mechanism manuscript and is no longer submission-current. A new journal-formatted package will be rebuilt and visually audited around the refocused Main before submission.
