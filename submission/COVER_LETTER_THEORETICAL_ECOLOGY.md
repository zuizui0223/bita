# Cover letter draft — Theoretical Ecology

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“From floral trait interactions to mechanism identification: a crossed-intervention framework for attraction and defence,”** as a Regular Article in *Theoretical Ecology*.

The paper addresses a general theoretical-ecology problem: a total interaction between two traits can be estimated while the ecological pathways generating that interaction remain structurally unidentified. Floral attraction and defence provide a concrete system because the same phenotype can change pollinator benefit, antagonist loss, and direct or allocation costs.

We define the experimentally measurable two-level interaction

`Delta_AD W = W11 - W10 - W01 + W00`

and distinguish the question **“does attraction-by-defence complementarity occur?”** from **“which channels generate that sign?”** The total interaction constrains an identified set of antagonist-relief, pollinator-interference and remaining joint-channel allocations. A crossed attraction × defence × antagonist × pollinator design then provides the selective contrasts needed to allocate the biotic channels, includes a four-way diagnostic for failure of channel separability, and requires an independent assay before the remaining residual is interpreted as joint cost.

The empirical stress tests sharpen rather than replace the theory. Kessler et al. (2008) already supplies a manipulated attraction-by-defence-like 2 × 2 field factorial. Published female-outcrossing summaries yield a robustly positive aggregate probability-scale interaction under our registered sensitivity analysis, but the source day/genotype uncertainty cannot be recovered from currently accessible supporting material. A public *Impatiens capensis* reanalysis supplies uncertainty-bearing observational attraction-by-defence estimates, but their intervals cross zero. The contrast between these cases exposes a precise identification frontier rather than a generic shortage of data.

We therefore separate outcome identification from mechanism allocation. A prospective Stage-1 replication can decide the total interaction with design-based uncertainty before an expensive 16-cell mechanism experiment is attempted. The repository now contains a registered power plan, plant/ICC allocation sensitivity, field-data contract, and block-bootstrap analysis that maps a Stage-1 interval directly to `ESCAPE_IDENTIFIED`, `ESCAPE_REFUTED`, or `ESCAPE_UNRESOLVED`. Channel-specific consumer interventions and an independent joint-cost assay are then added only for explaining the sign.

We believe this fits *Theoretical Ecology* because the manuscript uses an explicit theoretical identification problem to derive experimentally testable contrasts, partial-identification bounds, failure diagnostics, and a staged empirical programme. The framework is intended to transfer beyond flowers to ecological systems in which one phenotype affects several opposing pathways.

The manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere. Code, source-adjudication products, public-data reanalyses, registered sensitivity analyses, prospective-design receipts, and figure-generation workflows are maintained in the associated repository. Final author metadata, declarations, archival DOI, licence choice, and portal-controlled fields will be completed and approved by all authors before submission.
