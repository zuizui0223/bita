# Compact main tables for Ecology submission

These tables are a journal-packaging view of the canonical scientific tables in `manuscript/TABLES_THEORETICAL_ECOLOGY.md`. They preserve the frozen values and inference boundaries while moving exhaustive machine-readable detail to the Open Research package.

## Table 1. Mechanistic definitions and inference boundaries

| Term | Ecological meaning | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| \(A\) | One declared floral attraction trait | Not an omnibus attractiveness index |
| \(D\) | One declared flower-specific antagonist-reducing trait | Pollinator obstruction alone does not define defence |
| \(W_{AD}\) | Local attraction × defence mixed effect on the declared outcome | Does not imply positive marginal effects, covariance, or an evolutionary optimum |
| \(\rho\) | Antagonist-relief term | Requires the focal orientation/channel definition |
| \(\iota\) | Pollinator/mutualist-interference term | Not total pollination cost |
| \(\kappa\) | Direct joint-cost curvature | Not total energetic or construction cost |
| Selectivity window | \(\rho>\iota\) | Necessary for complementarity when \(\kappa\ge0\), not sufficient |
| \(P,H\) | Pollinator-service and antagonist-pressure context indices | Context effects need not act through only one channel |

**Note.** After the orientation gate, \(W_{AD}=\rho-\iota-\kappa\). The identity is bookkeeping; the one-sided bound is the structural result.
