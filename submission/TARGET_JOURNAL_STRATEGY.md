## Why this paper fits

The manuscript addresses a general ecological inference problem: a trait interaction can be detected before the pathways generating it are point-identified. Floral attraction and defence provide a tractable case because the same phenotype can alter pollination, antagonist damage, pollinator-independent reproduction, and direct allocation/construction channels.

The conceptual advance is an executable **identification ladder**:

1. define a directly measurable discrete A×D interaction;
2. represent compatible channel allocations as an identified set;
3. use explicit biological/channel restrictions for partial identification;
4. cross the same A/D coordinates with selective antagonist and pollinator interventions;
5. measure/correct the pollinator-absent A×D baseline;
6. use the A×D×G×P four-way interaction as a separability diagnostic;
7. keep the remaining joint channel unallocated until independent evidence gives it biological meaning.

The central partial-identification bound is

```text
kappa_delta >= 0
=> rho_delta - iota_delta >= Delta_AD W
```

and is presented only conditional on a defensible kappa restriction. The paper does not claim novelty for partial identification as a general causal-inference idea or for the one-line algebra itself.

## Mechanism → Pattern contribution

The source-adjudicated synthesis contains 56 route records from 25 independent biological clusters and covers all four marginal pathway families:

```text
A -> pollination: 5
A -> antagonism:  8
D -> antagonism: 18
D -> pollination: 10
same-system:      14
context switches: 17
```

These overlapping counts establish constituent-channel recurrence only.

The 16-system audit then reveals a **fragmented identification frontier**. Kessler 2008 is closest to the trait-factorial face; Egan 2021 to the consumer-factorial face; *Impatiens* reaches randomized context modification; *Pedicularis* supplies a selective-defence system anchor. No screened system closes all allocation dimensions or includes an independent joint-cost assay.

The stronger synthesis is therefore:

> **The constituent channels recur, existing studies constrain complementary parts of their allocation, and a total interaction can support explicit partial bounds, but full joint allocation still requires additional intervention.**

## Concepts & Synthesis positioning

Foreground five points:

1. **interaction detection ≠ point mechanism allocation**;
2. **non-identification is not all-or-none:** a total interaction plus explicit restrictions can yield partial identification;
3. **Mechanism → Pattern is empirical:** constituent pathways recur across systems;
4. **existing evidence is fragmented across complementary design faces** rather than simply absent;
5. **constructive endpoint:** choose the smallest augmentation that shrinks the identified set, with the selective 16-cell design as the route to point identification.

Do not sell the elementary algebra, context dependence, attraction–defence conflict, or partial identification in general as novel.
