## Part II — Pattern

### Figure 3. Meta-analytic pattern architecture and identification boundary

**Purpose:** answer the Part I mechanism with saturated cross-study patterns while preventing the reader from mistaking heterogeneous marginal evidence for an estimate of `W_AD`.

The committed figure must contain five distinct elements.

**A — Cross-study Pattern scaffold**

```text
A -> pollination        5 independent clusters
A -> antagonism         8
D -> antagonism        18
D -> pollination       10
56 effect/directional records
25 independent biological study clusters
```

This scaffold maps recurrence onto Part I mechanisms. It is **not a grand meta-analysis**, and overlapping route counts are not additive study totals.

**B — Recurrence and conditionality**

```text
same-system multi-route clusters: 14
context/sign-switch clusters:     17
context-only programs:             7  (excluded from route-ledger N)
```

The main recurrent conditionality states now include:

1. trait intensity / expression;
2. resource / exposure;
3. consumer identity / functional role;
4. response definition / decision stage / reproductive scale;
5. compound identity / mechanism partition;
6. guarded defence states;
7. spatial / temporal / attack-mode filtering;
8. visitor functional-mode routing;
9. lifecycle-stage role reversal;
10. population/site and trait-class dependence.

The seven context-only programs must be shown or described as excluded from the 25 route-ledger clusters; they are context evidence, not extra marginal-route N.

**C — Reproduced quantitative cross-study patterns**

- **Meta-analysis 1 — Leal et al. 2025:** female fitness LRR −0.210 (48 clusters), nectar −0.483 (28), visitation −0.291 (22); extreme heterogeneity retained.
- **Meta-analytic synthesis 2 — Sasidharan et al. 2023:** florivore 84/103 vs pollinator 151/220 physiological detections, assembled risk difference +0.129, LOCO positive 32/32; only three paired both-role components and all paired differences 0.

The two reproduced modules must remain visually separate because they do not share an effect-size scale.

**D — Secondary contextual syntheses**

Figure 3 carries a compact secondary-context strip rather than a third pooled meta-analysis:

- Haas-Desmarais et al. 2026 — 171 studies / 1,348 study cases; publisher supplement package verified; herbivory is not focal `D`;
- Caruso et al. 2019 — published selection synthesis, main analysis 755 gradients with SE from 36 articles; access-limited local reanalysis;
- Junker & Blüthgen 2010 — 18 publications / 425 floral-scent observations; consumer-dependence categories are not identical to pollinator/antagonist roles.

Their study/observation counts are not added to the 25 source-adjudicated route clusters and their scales are not pooled with Leal/Sasidharan.

**E — Identification bottlenecks**

```text
direct A x D:       1 strict cluster, sign unresolved
direct joint cost:  0 strict estimates, kappa unidentified
```

An explicit **IDENTIFICATION BOUNDARY** must separate the recurrent/meta-analytic Pattern evidence from direct identification of `W_AD`.
