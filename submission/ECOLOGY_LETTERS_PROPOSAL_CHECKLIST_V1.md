# Ecology Letters Synthesis proposal checklist v1

## Official route

Current Ecology Letters author guidance requires unsolicited Synthesis manuscripts to receive prior proposal approval.

Proposal route:

~~~text
article type: Synthesis
proposal limit: <= 300 words
send by email to:
  ecolets@cefe.cnrs.fr
  ecolets2@cefe.cnrs.fr
~~~

Proposal content should cover:

1. nature of the proposed work;
2. novelty;
3. contribution to the discipline;
4. author qualifications.

A conceptual figure or quantitative information may be included but is not mandatory.

Official author-guideline URL:

https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14610248/homepage/ForAuthors.html

Editorial guidance:

https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.14165

## Optional attachment

Preferred optional attachment:

~~~text
Figure 1 — Effective-exposure selectivity theory
~~~

The proposal already includes quantitative evidence, so the figure is optional rather than required.

If attached, use the latest CI-generated Figure 1 from the verified macro-figure workflow and do not attach the full manuscript unless requested.

## Scientific wording that must remain

Use:

> access and exposure domains organize interaction routing

Do not replace with:

- universal floral-defence law;
- causal network effect;
- domain > modality;
- all separated defences preserve pollination;
- tube length uniquely causes robbing.
